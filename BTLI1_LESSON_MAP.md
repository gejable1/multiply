### **L1 — Pananalangin** (Prayer) 🔒 **LOCKED — May 15, 2026**
- **Source:** USAD-L1 · Book 2 of the trilogy
- **Memory Verse:** **Jeremiah 29:12** — *"Tatawag kayo sa akin, paroroon kayo at mananalangin sa akin, at didinggin ko kayo."*
- **🌿 Character (Galatians):** **Faithfulness** — prayer is the discipline of *sustained relational presence with God*. Apostle Paul's "magsipanalangin kayong walang patid" (1 Thes 5:17) anchors this fruit; only Faithfulness captures the *non-stop* dimension of true prayer. The participant book reinforces it through the Jesus-as-model passages (Mark 1:35 busy days · Luke 6:12-13 decisions · John 11:41-42 crises · Mark 6:41 demands).
- **⚡ Competence (book-aligned, Claude-named):** ***ACTS-Anchored Conversational Prayer*** — moving from rote/episodic prayer ("emergency hotline") to sustained two-way conversation. The book frames this as: *"WALANG USAP, WALANG USAD. Walang COMMUNICATION, walang COMMUNION."* The competence is the rhythm itself: **ACTS** (Adoration · Confession · Thanksgiving · Supplication) governed by the **Lord's Prayer's 5 postures** (paggalang, pagpapasakop, pagdepende, kapatawaran, pag-adya).
- **LY Pair:** ✗ None
- **Pairing Confidence:** **Original** (book-content-driven; not Claude-invented)

### **L3 — Pagsuko** (Surrender / Consecration) 🔒 **LOCKED — May 15, 2026**
- **Source:** USAD-L3 · Book 2 of the trilogy
- **Memory Verse:** **Romans 12:1** — *"Kaya nga, mga kapatid, alang-alang sa masaganang habag ng Diyos sa atin, ako'y nakikiusap sa inyo na ialay ninyo ang inyong sarili bilang isang handog na buháy, banal at kalugud-lugod sa Diyos. Ito ang karapat-dapat na pagsamba ninyo sa Diyos."*
- **🌿 Character (Galatians):** **Joy** — *revised from skeleton (Self-Control → Joy) May 15, 2026.* Reading the USAD-L3 scan honestly, surrender is **never framed as self-control**. The lesson grounds surrender in Romans 12:1 ("alang-alang sa masaganang habag ng Diyos") — *responsive love to mercy received*, not gritted-teeth restraint. The lesson ends with the "I Surrender All" hymn whose climax is *"O the joy of full salvation!"* Joy is the paradoxical fruit of yielding — the joyful release of a burden too heavy to carry alone. This also relieves the Self-Control ×3 over-distribution flagged in the Galatians redistribution call (HANDOFF Priority D).
- **⚡ Competence (book-aligned, Claude-named):** ***Romans 12:1 Three-Sangkap Personal Inventory + Signed-Paper Commitment Ritual*** — the lesson explicitly walks through Romans 12:1 in three sangkap (sections): (1) **Masaganang Habag ng Dios** (the foundation — mercy already received), (2) **Malayang Handog ng Tao** (the response — free-will offering of self as living sacrifice), (3) **Malugod na Pagsamba at Paglilingkod** (the outflow — Monday-Saturday life as worship). The competence is the participant's ability to **name what mercy they've received, name what they're freely offering, and name how their week becomes worship** — then physically sign the blank-paper ritual from TUGON p.27 as a tangible commitment artifact.

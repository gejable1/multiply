# MULTIPLY PIPELINE DIAGRAM

**Rosehill Christian Church — 5-Level Leadership Pipeline**

**Purpose:** This file is the canonical reference for the 5-level Mac Lake leadership pipeline as adapted for Rosehill Christian Church. It defines each level, the FATS character filter, the training pathways (BTLI 101–106), the books, the roles, and the integration with the GRACE Pathway and Five Stages of Discipleship.

**Source:** Adapted from Mac Lake's *Leadership: The Multiplication Effect* and the six-book Mac Lake series (*Leading Yourself*, *Leading Others*, *Leading Leaders*, *Leading a Department/Ministry*, *Leading an Organization/Church*). Original concept lineage: Walt Mahler's Crossroads → Ram Charan's *The Leadership Pipeline* → Mac Lake's local-church adaptation.

**Last reviewed:** May 14, 2026 (initial extraction)

## 🧪 FATS — THE LEADERSHIP CHARACTER FILTER

Before advancing anyone past Level 1, the church looks for **FATS**:

- **F**aithful — Keeps their word. Consistent over time. Shows up. Does what they said they would do, when they said they would do it.
- **A**vailable — Has margin in their life. Is willing to be deployed. Says yes when asked, or says no honestly. Not over-committed and bitter.
- **T**eachable — Receives correction without defending. Asks questions. Reads what's recommended. Practices what's taught. Changes when shown a better way.
- **S**elfless — Uses authority to serve, not to be served. Doesn't grasp at titles. Promotes others. Stays small when no one's watching.

FATS is not a one-time test. It is the ongoing character pulse-check at every level. A leader can lose FATS — and when they do, they're pulled back to development, not advanced further.

---

## 📚 THE TRIAD OF DEVELOPMENT (Mac Lake)

Every level uses all three elements:

- **KNOWLEDGE** — what to know (lessons, books, BTLI, sermons)
- **EXPERIENCE** — what to practice (real ministry assignments, not simulations)
- **COACHING** — what to be corrected on (1-on-1 development conversations)

Knowledge or experience alone reinforces bad habits. Only the triad — all three working together — produces transformation. This triad governs the design of every BTLI module and every coaching pairing.

## 🌟 LEVEL 1 — TEAM MEMBER (Leads Yourself)

**Tagline:** *Participates in a group or team as a growing disciple.*

**5 Stages alignment:** Children
**GRACE alignment:** Anchor

### Core competencies (what they practice)
- Learning the Word (daily bible reading and devotional writing)
- Following Jesus (obedience to known commands)
- Loving the church (consistent LCG and Sunday attendance)
- Serving others (a place on a team in some ministry)
- Sharing the gospel (their first "one" in EOLO)
- Multiplying disciples (already beginning, even at this level)

### Eligible roles
Any ministry as a **team member**, **not as leader** — **except** Music, Teaching, and Preaching ministries (which require Level 2+ due to the public-trust nature of those roles).

### Training pathway
- **BTLI 101** — Leading Yourself
- **EGR (Experiencing God Retreat)** — now redesigned as evangelism for first-timers, but Team Members are encouraged to attend repeatedly and bring their "one"
- **Books:** *Usbong*, *Usad*

### Development focus
- Spiritual disciplines (Word, prayer, devotionals)
- Time stewardship
- Self-awareness (Strengths, DISC, Love Language, Enneagram, Conflict Style — full assessment battery in MMT)
- Accountability (LCG, mentor)

### Advancement criteria toward Level 2
- Consistent FATS character pattern observed over time
- Daily devotional practice established and sustained
- Active in an EOLO relationship
- LCG attendance habit
- Anchor 6-Module curriculum complete
- Baptism observed
- Demonstrated faithfulness in a team-member role
- Recommendation from LCG Leader and pastoral approval

## 🌟 LEVEL 2 — LEADER (Leads Others)

**Tagline:** *Provides leadership for a small team or group.*

**5 Stages alignment:** Young Adult
**GRACE alignment:** Cultivate (sub-level C2)

### Core competencies (what they practice)
- Mobilizing people
- Caring for people
- Delegating responsibility
- Confronting in love
- Encouraging leaders
- Periodic evaluation
- Situational leadership
- Solving problems

### Eligible roles
- **LC Leader** (the backbone of the church's discipleship structure)
- **Worship Leader**
- **Devotional Preacher**
- **Campus Minister**
- **Sunday School Teacher**

### Training pathway
- **BTLI 102 and 103** — Leading Others
- **IDC** training program
- **Books:** *Unlad*, *Fresh Wind Fresh Fire*

### Development focus
- Shepherding a small team
- Situational leadership (knowing when to direct, coach, support, delegate)
- Raising up apprentice leaders (already preparing the Level 3 transition)
- Problem-solving in real ministry contexts
- Conflict resolution

### Six Sources of Influence — applied
At Level 2, the leader begins consciously using multiple sources to drive transformation in their team members. Not all six at once — but at least 2–3 working together for any change initiative.

### Advancement criteria toward Level 3
- Demonstrated effective leadership of a team for a sustained season
- Apprentice leaders being developed (visible succession plan)
- Continued FATS character
- BTLI 102 and 103 completed
- Recommendation from a Level 3+ coach and pastoral approval

---

## 🌟 LEVEL 3 — COACH (Leads Leaders)

**Tagline:** *Encourages and guides a group of leaders within a ministry.*

**5 Stages alignment:** Young Adult (mature)
**GRACE alignment:** Cultivate (sub-level C3)

### Core competencies (what they practice)
- 1-to-1 development meetings with leaders
- Decision-making (at a ministry-area scope)
- Encouraging leaders
- Coaching for development (knowledge + experience + correction)

### Eligible roles
- **Sunday Preacher**
- **Seminar Lecturer**
- **Trainor**
- **Council Member**
- **BTLI Lecturer**

### Training pathway
- **BTLI 104** — Leading Leaders
- **IDC** advanced track
- **APIIS** core program
- **Clifton Strengths** certification or deep study
- **Books:** *Mere Christianity*, *Still Sovereign*, *Happy Lies*

### Development focus
- 1-on-1 development conversation skill
- Recognizing leader-stage problems vs follower-stage problems
- Discernment in promoting (and not promoting) people
- Theological depth (this is where preaching-track preparation deepens)

### For future preachers specifically
- **CCF Glorious Hope program**
- **APIIS Masters Program** (for those who will preach as their primary calling)

### Advancement criteria toward Level 4
- Multiple Level 2 leaders developed under their care, currently leading effectively
- Demonstrated ability to set direction for a ministry area, not just shepherd individuals
- Theological clarity (especially for preaching-track)
- BTLI 104 completed
- Recommendation from Level 4+ and senior pastoral approval

## 🌟 LEVEL 4 — DIRECTOR (Leads a Department / Ministry)

**Tagline:** *Sets vision, structure, and systems for a ministry area.*

**5 Stages alignment:** Parent
**GRACE alignment:** Extend (sub-level E1)

### Core competencies (what they practice)
- Casting vision for a ministry
- Building structure and systems
- Coaching the Coaches under them
- Resource stewardship
- Cross-ministry collaboration
- Multi-year planning

### Eligible roles
- **Ministry Leader / Director**
- **Life Coach** (formal coaching role within the church's coaching program)
- **Core Group Member**

### Training pathway
- **BTLI 105** — Leading a Department
- **IDC Coaching Training**
- **Counseling** foundations (basic pastoral care competencies)
- **Books:** *Leadership Coaching* by Tony Stoltzfus; *The Disciplines, Skills and Heart of a Christian Coach* by Keith E. Webb

### Development focus
- Designing systems that reproduce leaders (not just programs that produce outputs)
- Six Sources of Influence applied to ministry-area design
- Healthy delegation (releasing, not just assigning)
- Spotting Level 3 → Level 4 candidates

### Advancement criteria toward Level 5
- Their ministry produces reproducing leaders — visible 2-generation discipleship (their disciples have disciples)
- Demonstrated cross-ministry collaboration without territorial behavior
- BTLI 105 completed
- Recommendation from existing Executive Leaders and senior pastoral approval (with discernment around calling, not just competence)

## 🌟 LEVEL 5 — EXECUTIVE LEADER (Leads an Organization / Church)

**Tagline:** *Sets direction, model, and culture for the whole organization.*

**5 Stages alignment:** Parent (mature, grandparent generation)
**GRACE alignment:** Extend (sub-level E2)

### Core competencies (what they practice)
- Persevering past barriers
- Leading meetings well
- Improving systems
- Setting the model and culture
- Developing other Executive Leaders
- Stewarding the broader mission

### Eligible roles
- **Pastor**
- **Missionary**
- **Church Planter**

### Training pathway
- **BTLI 106** — Leading an Organization
- **IDC**
- **Church Planting Training**
- **Books:** *Why Men Hate Going to Church*; *Christian Coaching: Helping Others Turn Potential into Reality* (2nd ed.)

### Development focus
- Becoming the model (the culture you carry is the culture the church will catch)
- 2 Tim 2:2 legacy planning ("entrust to reliable people")
- Naming your 2–3 successors and investing in them deliberately
- Holding the long view (decade-scope, not quarter-scope)

### Non-advancement: completion
Level 5 has no further "promotion." The work is multiplication — sending out planters, missionaries, and other Executive Leaders. A Level 5 who has not produced other Level 5s has not yet finished the work.

## 🔗 KEY ALIGNMENT PRINCIPLES (Mac Lake)

### **The leadership development is a discipleship pathway**
The pipeline is not a corporate ladder. It is a **disciple-making structure**. Every level is about following Jesus more fully, not climbing higher.

### **Competencies cluster at Leader-level by ministry, but unify above**
The competencies at **Level 2 (Leader)** can vary by ministry (a worship leader needs different competencies than an LC leader). But from **Level 3 (Coach)** upward, the competencies are largely the same regardless of ministry domain. This is why BTLI 104+ becomes more universal.

### **Hire / promote from within**
Where possible, leaders are developed from inside the pipeline rather than imported. As one Mac Lake-quoted leader put it: *"If we hire from within, we don't have to train someone in our culture and values, and we are able to focus on task development."*

### **Adding value to all of life**
Pipeline development is not just for church roles. The competencies a person develops as a Level 3 Coach in the church translate directly to their role as a parent, spouse, professional, neighbor, and community member. **People value what adds value to them** — and when leadership development bears fruit in every domain, members lean into the process.

## 🚦 PIPELINE MOVEMENT — HOW PROMOTION ACTUALLY WORKS

Movement through the pipeline is **never automatic** and never primarily based on time-in-stage. The required combination at every transition:

1. **Character (FATS)** — sustained over a meaningful season
2. **Competence** — demonstrated, not just trained-in
3. **Capacity** — the person has actual margin to take on more
4. **Calling** — internal sense of pull, confirmed by external observation
5. **Confirmation** — the next level above plus pastoral approval

Any of the five missing = wait, don't promote.

---

## 🎉 CELEBRATION AT EACH LEVEL

Every level transition is celebrated publicly. The church culture treats spiritual leadership advancement the way other communities treat athletic championships or academic graduations — visibly, joyfully, narratively.

Suggested celebration patterns:
- **Level 1 → Level 2:** Commissioning during a Sunday service; LCG Leader's prayer over the new leader
- **Level 2 → Level 3:** Coach commissioning; mentoring assignment formally announced
- **Level 3 → Level 4:** Director installation; ministry area officially entrusted
- **Level 4 → Level 5:** Sending — for missionary, church planter, or pastoral role

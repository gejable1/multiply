Lesson DNA
The Big Idea — Read This First
5 min · Before opening any slide
▼

The session in one sentence: A disciple becomes a multiplier when their strengths + passions + calling align with God's purpose — and that alignment is sustained by joy rooted in faith, not in circumstances.

What changes inside a participant by the end of 90 minutes:

  • They see: Purpose is built into design — even in things they thought were useless. Including their own life.
  • They understand: Mac Lake's framework — Strengths + Passion + Calling = Mission. Mac Lake's character anchor — Joy is the fuel that keeps mission alive through hardship.
  • They do: Draft a one-sentence Life Mission Statement. Self-rate on the 5 Living-on-Mission proficiencies. Commit to a 10-day mission-on-the-ground journal.
🎯 The One Movement That Matters Most
GAWIN. If TALAKAY's reflection gets cut short — fine. If DALHIN gets compressed — fine. But they must leave with a written first draft of their mission statement. That is the artifact this lesson produces. Everything else serves it.
Source Acknowledgment

This lesson synthesizes Mac Lake's Leading Yourself Module 1 (Living on Mission) — Joy as character, Strengths + Passion + Calling — with USAD Lesson 1 (Fulfilling God's Purpose) — the lungfish hook, Acts 13:36 as anchor, the Whitney Houston cautionary contrast. Mac Lake's downloadable worksheets (Rear View Mirror + Life Mission Statement) at multiplygroup.org/leadingyourself are pointed to in DALHIN for participants who want to go deeper.

🎯 Intern Pre-Session Briefing — 15 min before (Cohort only)
Gather all interns. Cover: (1) The main truth of today — strengths+passion+calling=mission, sustained by joy. (2) Walk through the lungfish PUKAW activity physically — they need to have done it themselves before facilitating it. (3) The two pastoral moments not to rush — the 4-minute silent reflection in TALAKAY and the Mission Statement Sketch in GAWIN. Movement 1 · PUKAW
Engage — Create Felt Need
12 minutes · Session opens here
▼
The Hook — African Lungfish (Slide 2)

Play the African Lungfish clip (NatGeo UK, ~2 min). The fish that lives in mud, breathes air, and survives droughts of up to four years. Looks useless. Looks like a failure of design. Is actually one of God's most surprising engineering feats.

Then tease the Brightside "12 Common Things Whose True Purpose You Didn't Know" list — bottle-cap ridges, jeans coin-pocket, the hole in pen caps, those weird tabs on phone chargers. Everyone thought they knew. Almost nobody did.

🎯 The Setup — Why This Hook
You are not teaching biology. You are setting up a single pivot: "If even the small, simple things have purposes nobody noticed — what about your life?" Resist the urge to over-explain the lungfish. The image does the work; the question does the lifting.
Cohort Activity 5 TABLES
⚡ Manila Paper Activity — "Akala Ko vs Tunay Pala"
Tables × Gallery Walk × Pivot (10 min)
Setup (1 min): Each table has Manila paper folded into 2 columns: "Akala ko gamit nito ay ___" and "Ang tunay na gamit pala ay ___."

Round 1 (4 min): Each table picks 3 items from the projected list (or invents their own). Fill both columns. Make it funny. Make it surprising.

Gallery walk (3 min): Tables rotate clockwise. Read other tables' lists. Laugh. Tape sticky notes with reactions on lists you didn't see coming.

The Pivot (2 min) — you take the floor: Project Slide 3. "Kung ang mga simpleng bagay may layunin na hindi natin alam — paano pa kaya ang isang tao? Paano pa kaya ang buhay mo?"
Small Group Activity 1 TABLE
⚡ Round-Robin Wonder — "Akala Ko vs Tunay Pala"
One Table, Shared Manila Paper (8 min)
Setup (1 min): One Manila paper at the center, same 2-column fold. Markers passed around.

Round-robin (5 min): Each person, in order, names ONE item from the list (or anything else) and fills both columns. No repeats. Encourage laughter. Encourage the person who's been quiet to go second or third.

The Pivot (2 min): Project or hold up Slide 3. Same line: "Kung ang simpleng bagay may layunin na hindi natin alam — paano pa kaya ang buhay mo?" Let it land. Don't fill the silence.
1-on-1 Conversation MENTOR-MENTEE
⚡ Dialogue Hook — Phone + Question
Watch · Wonder · Wait (10 min)
Watch (2 min): Pull up the lungfish video on your phone. Watch it together. No commentary while it plays.

Wonder (3 min): Casual question — "Anong reaction mo sa nakita natin? Anong na-realize mo kahit isang sandali lang?" Listen. Don't lecture.

Wait — the pivot question (5 min): Lean in slightly. Lower your voice. Ask: "Anong bagay sa buhay mo ang akala mo ay walang silbi — pero may purpose pala? O baka hindi mo pa nakikita ang purpose nito?" Then wait. The first 30 seconds of silence is the most important. Don't rescue.
Bridge Script (same for all modes)
🎤 Your own words — taglish is natural here
"Lahat ng ginawa ng Diyos may layunin. Pati ang nawawalang lungfish sa putik. Pati ang butas sa takip ng bolpen. Pati — at ito ang importanteng tanong ngayon — ang buhay mo. Hindi ka aksidente. Hindi ka spare part. May ginawa ang Diyos sa iyo bago ka pa ipanganak. Ang tanong: alam mo na ba kung ano iyon? At kung alam mo na — pinamumuhay mo ba?"

Movement 2 · TUKLAS
Discover — Encounter the Truth
20 minutes · Guide discovery, do not lecture
▼
Three Scriptures (Slides 4-6)
Acts 13:36 · Primary Anchor (ESV)
"Sapagkat si David, matapos niyang paglingkuran ang layunin ng Diyos sa kanyang sariling henerasyon, ay natulog..."
"For David, after he had served the purpose of God in his own generation, fell asleep..."
Ephesians 2:10 · The Poiema Verse (ESV)
"Sapagkat tayo ay Kanyang gawa, nilikha kay Cristo Jesus para sa mabubuting gawa, na inihanda ng Diyos noon pa man upang sundin natin."
"For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them."
John 15:11 · The Joy Anchor (ESV)
"Ang mga bagay na ito ay sinabi Ko sa inyo, upang ang Aking kagalakan ay sumainyo, at ang inyong kagalakan ay maging ganap."
"These things I have spoken to you, that my joy may be in you, and that your joy may be full."
📖 Reading Method — 3 Texts, 3 Voices
Acts 13:36 — you read in Tagalog, one participant reads in English. Ephesians 2:10 — read together aloud, both languages back-to-back (Tagalog first, then English). John 15:11 — one volunteer reads, then everyone reads silently a second time. Three different rhythms keeps it from feeling like a recitation drill.
Cohort Discovery 5 TABLES · 12 MIN
  • OBSERVATION (Acts 13:36): "Sa isang pangungusap lang, paano ibinuod ng Bibliya ang buong buhay ni David? Ilista sa Manila paper ang exact na salita o parirala." Guide toward: "served the purpose of God in his own generation" — pinaglingkuran ang layunin ng Diyos sa kanyang sariling henerasyon.
  • INTERPRETATION (Eph 2:10): "Ang Griyegong salitang ginamit dito para sa 'workmanship' ay poiema — mula dito nagmula ang English na poem. Masterpiece. Tula. Ano ang ipinapahiwatig nito tungkol sa kung paano ka tiningnan ng Diyos noong ginawa ka Niya?" Guide toward: intentional · custom-designed · the good works were prepared BEFOREHAND, not improvised.
  • APPLICATION (John 15:11): "Ang joy ayon kay Jesus ay nagiging 'ganap' kapag tayo ay nakakabit sa Kanya tulad ng sanga sa puno ng ubas. Ano ang gusto sabihin nito tungkol sa kaugnayan ng misyon at kagalakan?" Guide toward: mission without joy = burnout · joy without mission = shallow · the two are designed to feed each other.
📝 Gallery Wall
"Ang Misyon ng Diyos sa Akin ay..."
Each table writes ONE sentence on Manila paper: their best first-draft answer. Tape on the wall. 2-minute walk — read 4-5. Facilitator reads 2 aloud and affirms. Makes the discovery visible and communal.
Small Group Discovery ROUND-ROBIN · 12 MIN

Same three questions — but round-robin around the table. Each person answers ONE question (rotate so everyone gets one). Then open table discussion for 4-5 minutes drawing out the patterns.

  • OBSERVATION (Acts 13:36): Same as Cohort.
  • INTERPRETATION (Eph 2:10) — poiema: Same as Cohort.
  • APPLICATION (John 15:11) — misyon at kagalakan: Same as Cohort.
🎯 Small-Group Dynamics Note
Watch for the person who hasn't spoken yet — gently invite them in: "[Name], anong naintindihan mo sa Eph 2:10?" No pressure if they pass. The point is participation, not performance.
1-on-1 Discovery DIALOGUE · 12 MIN

Read each scripture aloud to your mentee, slowly. Then ask the question and listen more than you teach. Your job is not to deliver three theological points — it is to help your mentee discover what's already true.

  • Acts 13:36: "Kung ang buong buhay mo ay ibubuod sa isang pangungusap pagkamatay mo — anong sinasabi mong sasabihin? Hindi ang ibig sabihin nais mo — kundi ang aktwal na sasabihin ng kasalukuyan mong buhay."
  • Eph 2:10: "Kung tula ka ng Diyos — hindi sketch, hindi draft, kundi masterpiece — paano nito binabago ang pagtingin mo sa iyong sarili kapag nahihirapan ka?"
  • John 15:11: "Saan mo huling naranasan ang tunay na kagalakan — hindi 'happy', kundi joy? Ano ang gawain o sandaling iyon? Maaari kayang konektado iyon sa kung sino ka talaga?"
🎯 1-on-1 Pacing
Do NOT speed through all three. If your mentee is opening up on the first question, stay there. Better to go deep on one than skim three. The point is encounter, not coverage.
Bridge to TALAKAY
🎤 Draw out the main truth
"Nakikita ninyo? Ang Diyos ay hindi nag-improvise sa iyo. Ikaw ay poiema — masterpiece na may inihandang gawa pa noon pa man. At ang pinakamalalim na kagalakan — ang ganap na kagalakan — ay nadarama lamang kapag pinamumuhay mo ang ginawang-purpose mo. Pero paano natin malalaman kung ano ito? Iyon ang pag-uusapan natin sa susunod."

Movement 3 · TALAKAY
Deepen — Truth Becomes Personal
20 minutes · Teaching + Contrast + Reflection
▼
Part A — The Mac Lake Framework (6 min · Slide 7)
Living on Mission — Mac Lake's Definition
"Understanding your strengths and passions and aligning them with God's unique calling for your life so that your actions and decisions reflect your commitment to his purpose."

The three components:

① Strengths — anong likas at marubdob na kakayahan? Hindi lang "magaling ka rito" — kundi "nakakapagod ka sa ibang gawain, pero dito ikaw ay nag-i-energize." Iyon ang signal ng strength.

② Passion — anong sumusunog sa puso mo? Anong injustice, kakulangan, o pangangailangan ang hindi mo maiwasang pansinin? Iyon ang clue sa puso ng Diyos para sa iyo.

③ Calling — anong tinatawag ng Diyos para sa iyong henerasyon (Acts 13:36)? Hindi calling para sa abstract na "ministry" — calling para sa specific na panahon, lugar, mga tao.

Kapag nagkrus ang tatlo — mission zone. Sa labas ng krus na iyon — burnout, drift, o pagkawalang-pakialam.

🎯 6 Minutes Only — Don't Over-Teach
Project the framework. Say each piece once. Use ONE personal example from your own life — preferably brief, brief, brief. The participants don't need a sermon. They need just enough framework to start sketching their own mission statement in GAWIN. Save your stories for one-on-one follow-up.
Part B — The Cautionary Contrast (6 min · Slide 8)
🕊 Honor the Gift · Name the Cost
Whitney Houston — Strengths + Passion · Misaligned Calling

Si Whitney Houston ay may isa sa mga pinaka-pambihirang boses na nakilala ng mundo. Tunay na strength. Lumaki siyang umaawit ng gospel sa simbahan kasama ang kanyang ina at tiya — tunay na passion.

Pero sa kasaysayan, may dalawang Whitney lamang. Hindi tatlo. Ang ikatlong elemento — ang calling, ang inihandang gawa ng Diyos sa kanyang henerasyon — ay unti-unting napalitan ng karera, ng entertainment industry, ng addiction. Strength: oo. Passion: oo, pero nagdrift. Calling: nawala sa daan.

Hindi natin siya kinokondena. Hinahangaan natin ang regalo. Iginagalang natin ang kanyang ugat sa simbahan. Pero ipinapakita ng kanyang kuwento ang isang katotohanan: dalawa sa tatlo ay hindi sapat. Ang strengths + passion na walang calling ay maaaring maging tanyag — ngunit hindi maaaring maging tapos tulad ni David.

🎯 Pastoral Tone — Critical
Some participants will love her music. Some saw her live. Do not condemn. Do not moralize. Speak with gravity and gentleness. The point is not "Whitney was bad" — the point is "any of us can have two of three and miss the third without noticing." That includes the people in this room. That includes you.
Part C — Silent Reflection (8 min)
💡 4-Minute Silent Reflection COHORT
"Saan ako ngayon?"
Setup: Project Slide 9 with the three questions. Distribute Manila-paper or just use the participant page on phones.

The 4-Minute Silence (4 min): Real silence. No music. No facilitator filler. You walk slowly around the room — calm, prayerful presence.

Optional voluntary share (4 min): "May gustong magbahagi ng isang pangungusap tungkol sa nakikita ninyo? Hindi lahat — ilan lang." Receive 2-3 honest shares. Then close: "Maraming salamat sa inyong katapatan. Ngayon, gagawin natin ito sa papel."
💡 Paired Reflection SMALL GROUP
"Saan ako ngayon?" — Pairs Within the Group
Setup (1 min): Project the three questions on Slide 9.

Individual writing (3 min): Silent. On the participant page or notebook.

Pair share (4 min): Pair off (the person next to you). Each shares ONE answer of their choosing (any of the three). The partner listens — only allowed to ask "Sabihin mo pa" if it helps. No advice.
💡 Mentor-Mentee Dialogue 1-on-1
The Three Questions — Slow Walk
You ask each of the three questions, one at a time. Wait for the answer. Reflect it back: "Kung tama ang pagkakarinig ko sa iyo — ang sinasabi mo ay ___?" Then move to the next question. Take 7-8 minutes for all three. Don't rush.
The Three Reflection Questions (Slide 9 · all modes)
  • Ang Aking Strengths: Sa nakaraang ilang linggo, anong gawain o aktibidad ang nag-e-energize sa akin? At anong aktibidad ang nag-d-drain sa akin?
  • Ang Aking Passion: Anong isyu, pangangailangan, o grupo ng tao ang lagi kong iniisip — kahit hindi ko sinusubukang isipin?
  • Ang Aking Calling: Para sa aking henerasyon — ano kaya ang ginawa ng Diyos sa akin? Anong inihandang gawa na hindi pa naisasakatuparan?
🎯 The 4-Minute Silence — Do Not Break It (Cohort)
Filipino group culture is deeply uncomfortable with silence. Resist the urge to fill it with music, words, or movement. Walk slowly. Your calm presence gives permission for genuine reflection. If someone cries — that is the Spirit, not failure. A quiet nod is enough. Do not interrupt.

Movement 4 · GAWIN
Practice — Do Something With the Truth
20 minutes · The Artifact-Producing Movement
▼
Part A — Rear View Mirror Mini (5 min · Slide 10)
🪞 Rear View Mirror — 7-Day Edition
Adapted from Mac Lake's Leading Yourself · multiplygroup.org/leadingyourself
Each participant opens the participant page → TALAKAY section. Two columns: "Nag-Energize" and "Nag-Drain". Sa nakaraang 7 araw — magsulat ng top 3 sa bawat hanay. Hindi top 10. Top 3 lang. Forces clarity.

Pattern question: "Tingnan ang Energize column. Anong tema ang nakikita mo? Iyon ang clue sa iyong top 2-3 strengths."

Why mini: Mac Lake's full Rear View Mirror is a 30-45 minute solo exercise covering 2 weeks. We do 7 days for an in-session start. Encourage them to do the full version at home from the multiplygroup.org link.
Part B — Life Mission Statement Sketch (10 min · Slide 11)
🎯 First Draft — The Artifact of This Lesson
Filipino-Context Template
Project Slide 11. The template:

"Ang aking misyon ay [strengths] na ibinigay sa akin ng Diyos para sa [passion/people] sa pamamagitan ng [paraan] — para sa Kanyang kaluwalhatian."

Three blanks. Three minutes per blank. The participant page has live preview that assembles the full sentence as they type.

The artifact this lesson produces: Every participant leaves with a first-draft mission statement. Not perfect. Not final. First draft. They will revise it across the next 5 lessons.
Cohort Pairing PARTNER ROLEPLAY
🙌 Mission Read-Back · Pairs at Each Table
  1. Each person reads their first-draft mission statement aloud to a partner at their table
  2. Partner gives ONE affirmation: "Ang pinaka-clear na parte ng misyon mo ay ___."
  3. Partner gives ONE question: "Anong ibig sabihin mo sa ___?" (curious, not critical)
  4. Switch. Repeat. (Total: 6 min · 3 min each direction)
  5. Table debrief — Table Coach asks: "Sino ang natuklasan na may bagong claridad? 1-2 shares per table."
Small Group Round EVERYONE SHARES
🙌 Round-Robin Read · Whole Group
  1. Each person reads their first-draft mission statement aloud — everyone hears it
  2. The group gives ONE affirmation and ONE curious question per person
  3. Approximately 90 seconds per person — at 8-12 people, this is 12-18 min total but worth it
  4. Facilitator closes: "Sa next session, dalhin ninyo ang inyong revised version."
Mentor Conversation DEEP READ
🙌 Mentor as First Hearer of the Mission Statement
  1. Your mentee reads their first-draft mission statement aloud to you
  2. You reflect it back in your own words: "Kung tama ang naintindihan ko — sinasabi mo na ___."
  3. One affirmation: "Ang resonates sa akin ay ___ — dahil nakita ko ito sa iyo noong ___."
  4. One curious question: "Kapag sinabi mong ___ — anong specific picture sa isip mo?"
  5. Pray together over the statement — naming the strengths, the passion, the calling specifically
Part C — EOLO Bridge (5 min · Slide 12)
🌏 EOLO + Mission Connection
  1. "Sa session pa lamang tayo, pero gusto kong itanong: mayroon ba kayong isang tao sa isip ninyo na nais ninyong mahalin papunta kay Cristo?"
  2. Participants write the name in the EOLO section of their participant page
  3. The connection: "Paano kaya magagamit ang inyong bagong mission statement para mahalin ang taong iyon? Strengths + passion + paraan = paano ko siya seserbisyuhan?"
  4. Participants write this in the EOLO mission box
  5. (Cohort) Table coaches lead 60-second specific prayer per table. (Small Group) Group prays together by name. (1-on-1) Mentor prays specifically for both the mentee and their EOLO person.
🎯 Why EOLO Sits Inside GAWIN, Not as an Add-On
The mission statement is not abstract — it must serve real people. Connecting it to EOLO in the same movement makes the abstraction land in flesh-and-blood relationships. The participant leaves with: a mission statement + one specific person + one specific way to live it out toward them. That is the disciplemaking culture made operational.

The 10-Day Assignment (Slide 13)
🏠 Takdang-Aralin — 10 Araw
Buhay May Misyon — Daily Witness Journal
"Sa susunod na sampung araw — bawat gabi bago matulog — magsulat ng isang pangungusap sa participant page:

'Ngayon, naglingkod ako sa layunin ng Diyos sa pamamagitan ng ___.'

Kahit maliit. Lalo na maliit. Hindi mo kailangan ng dramatic na ministry moment. Pwedeng natulungan mo lang ang katabi mo sa MRT. Pwedeng makinig ka lang sa kapatid mo. Pwedeng na-resist mo ang isang bagay na bumabaling ka palayo sa misyon. Lahat iyon ay paglilingkod sa Kanyang layunin.

Sa susunod na lesson, ang unang tanong: 'Ilang araw ka nakapagsulat?' Walang shame — datos lang."

The Closing Ritual — Same Every Lesson
🕊 Closing · 3 Minutes · Slide 18 · SACRED — DO NOT REDESIGN
  1. Everyone stands
  2. Memory verse together from memory — final time
  3. You pray specifically — name 2-3 participants and their EOLO person and their first-draft mission
  4. Everyone together, hands raised: "Disciples who make disciples."
  5. (Cohort) Table Coaches dismiss their tables — remind of next session date. (SG/1-on-1) Confirm next meeting time.
📋 Post-Session Intern Debrief — 10 min (Cohort only)
Three questions only: (1) "Sino ang pinaka-memorable na kalahok ngayon — bakit?" (2) "Anong moment ang pinaka-makapangyarihan sa inyong mesa?" (3) "Anong pastoral concern ang kailangan nating bantayan?" Write down names. Pray together. These feed into Lesson 2 planning.

The Big Idea (Slide)
🎯 Pangunahing Katotohanan
"Imposibleng lumalim ang pagmamahal sa Dios kung hindi lumalim ang pagmamahal sa Kaniyang Salita."
Anchor Scripture
Awit 1:2-3 · Memory Verse
"...kasiyahan niyang sumunod sa kautusan ni Yahweh. Binubulay-bulay niya ito sa araw at gabi. Katulad niya'y punongkahoy sa tabi ng isang batisan, laging sariwa ang dahon at namumunga sa takdang panahon."
"...whose delight is in the law of the LORD, and who meditates on his law day and night. That person is like a tree planted by streams of water, which yields its fruit in season and whose leaf does not wither."
Joshua 1:8
"Huwag mong kaliligtaang basahin ang aklat ng kautusan. Pagbulay-bulayan mo iyon araw at gabi upang matupad mo ang lahat ng nakasaad doon."
Define Bible Meditation (use the cow image)

Devotion — Ancient Latin: "pagtatalaga o pag-aalay ng sarili na bunga ng matinding pagtatapat at pagmamahal." Not a ritual. Not an assignment. Pag-aalay.

Meditation — Hebrew: "mamutawi lagi sa labi." Walang ibang bukambibig kundi ang Salita ng Dios.

The picture: Rumination. Cows have 4 stomachs. Kumain, ngumuya, lulunukin, isusuka ulit, ngunguya muli — paulit-ulit hanggang ma-absorb ang nutrients. Iyan ang Bible meditation. Paulit-ulit na pagbasa, pagnamnam, paglago.

Contrast Hindu meditation (empty the mind) with Biblical meditation (fill the mind with God's thoughts).

5 Roles ng Bible (Walk through quickly)

Each role: 1 verse, 1 sentence, 1 image. Don't over-teach. Participants have it in their booklet.

  • 1. Katotohanan · John 17:3 · panlaban sa kasinungalingan ng diablo
  • 2. Spiritual Food · Matt. 4:4 · tinapay pang-araw-araw, hindi cake ("kahit si Manny Pacquiao kaya mong talunin kung gugutumin mo ng ilang linggo")
  • 3. Sandata/Sword · Heb. 4:12 · offensive weapon, ginamit ni Hesus laban kay Satan
  • 4. Salamin · James 1:22-23 · ire-reveal ang lahat ng dapat baguhin
  • 5. Tanglaw at Liwanag · Awit 119:105 · security, direction sa kadiliman
Reflection (to participant booklet)
🎤 90-second silent reflection
"Sa 5 roles ng Bible, ano ang pinakakailangan mo ngayon? Bakit? Isulat sa booklet niyo. Walang sasagutin ng malakas — para ito sa Dios at sa'yo."
🎯 Pastoral Note — What to Watch For
Watch which role gets chosen by people who have been Christians for years vs. by newer believers. Longtime believers often pick Mirror (they need conviction) or Sword (they're in a fight). Newer believers often pick Spiritual Food (they're hungry) or Tanglaw (they're disoriented). Note who is in which season — pastoral data for follow-up.

Discuss — 4 Soils + Tozer Question
~12 minutes · Table discussion, brief whole-room share
▼
Frame the 4 Soils

Tell the parable briefly (Matt. 13). Same seed. Different soil. Different outcomes. Why? Iba-iba ang response.

  • Tabing-Kalsada (v.4,19) — hindi nakatago ang Salita; agad naagaw ng diablo
  • Mabato (v.5-6,20-21) — tumanggap masaya, mababaw ang ugat, tumalikod sa hirap
  • Matinik (v.7,22) — hati ang atensyon dahil sa kamunduhan
  • Mataba (v.8,23) — tumanggap, tumugon, tumalima — sumagana
Table Discussion Questions
📋 Tables of 4-6 · 7 minutes
  1. Anong klaseng lupa ang puso mo ngayon? Bakit?
  2. Ano ang pangunahing dahilan kung bakit minsan hindi nakakapag-devotion ang isang believer? (Reasons na common sa lahat.)
  3. Sang-ayon ka ba kay A.W. Tozer: "Whatever keeps me from my Bible is my enemy, however harmless it may appear to me"? Bakit o bakit hindi?
Brief Whole-Room Share (3-4 min)

1-2 voluntary shares from a couple of tables. Don't force participation. The point is normalizing the struggle, not extracting confessions.

Set Up the Samuel Posture
1 Samuel 3:10 · The devotion-opener prayer
"Mangusap po kayo, nakikinig ang inyong lingkod."

Teach this as the devotion-opener: "Before you open the Bible tomorrow, before any caffeine, idasal mo lang ito — 'Mangusap po kayo, nakikinig ang inyong lingkod.' Iisang linya. Iyan ang humihingi sa Espiritu na buksan ang isip mo bago mo buksan ang libro."

⚠️ The Prince of Grenada caution
Tell this near the end of TALAKAY: A prisoner in Grenada was kept in solitary for 33 years with nothing but a Bible. When he died, his cell walls were covered with Bible trivia — chapter counts, longest word, number of times a name appears. But no evidence he ever met God. Information without transformation. "Huwag lang sanang lumalim ang pagkaalam natin sa Salita ng Dios kundi lumalim ang pagkakilala natin sa Dios."

The Passage
Filipos 4:6-7 · In-group practice text
"Huwag kayong mabalisa tungkol sa anumang bagay. Sa halip, sa lahat ng pagkakataon ay manalangin kayo, at hingin sa Diyos ang inyong mga kailangan, nang may pagpapasalamat. Why this passage: It pairs with L1 (prayer) — it's literally a verse about prayer dissolving anxiety. Continuity from last lesson. Hindi accidental ang choice.

The 5-Box S.O.A.P.S Walk-through
⚡ Hands-On · 10 min
Subukan sa Buong Grupo — Punuin ang Lahat ng 5 Boxes
Setup: Each pax has the 5-box S.O.A.P.S sheet. (Note: ang printed template sa libro ay 4-box. Ang TUGON narrative ay nag-define ng 5 letras. Honoring the richer version.)

Walk through one box at a time. Give 90 seconds per box. Do not move on until interns confirm tables are tracking.

S — Scripture · Isulat ang isa o dalawang verses na pumukaw sa puso.
O — Observation · Sino nagsasalita? Saan? Bakit? Ano ang context?
A — Application · Paano mo ito i-aapply ngayong linggo? Specific.
P — Prayer · Maikling panalangin bilang tugon.
S — Sharing · Kanino mo ito puwedeng i-share? Hint: isa sa EOLO names mo.
🎯 The Sharing Letter — Why It Matters
The original printed template (© 2020 Loving Christ Ministries, p.20) shows 4 boxes — S/O/A/P. The participant book narrative (p.19 TUGON) describes 5 letters — S/O/A/P/S. We honor the richer narrative version. Sharing is the letter that welds Bible meditation directly to EOLO mission. Without it, S.O.A.P. risks becoming a private discipline. With it, every devotion becomes a potential gospel conversation.
During the Try — What Interns Do
  1. Sit at tables. Model filling a box if anyone is stuck.
  2. Watch for the one or two who are writing fast vs. staring at the page. Quiet ones may need a soft prompt.
  3. If someone asks "ano ipapasulat ko?" — say: "Anuman ang nakatakas sa atensyon mo sa unang basa. May verse na napansin mong nag-iba ka? Iyan."
  4. For Application — push for specifics. "Ngayong linggo" not "in the future." "Sa asawa ko" not "sa ibang tao."
  5. For Sharing — gently nudge: "Tingnan mo ang EOLO list mo. Sino ang naka-rate?"

The 30-Day S.O.A.P.S Challenge

🪞 Debrief — 15-20 min within 48 hours of L2
Pastor + facilitator + interns gather. Be honest. The point is to see what happened — not to grade.
For the Facilitator
1Ilang pax actually nagpalakas ng kamay sa prayer-pact callback? Ano ang sinabi sa'yo ng numerong iyon?
Honest count. If <30%, L1 didn't stick — note it for the LCG follow-up. If >70%, celebrate publicly next Sunday.
2Anong role of the Bible (Truth/Food/Sword/Mirror/Light) ang pinakamadalas napiling pinakakailangan ngayon?
Iyong pattern ay pastoral data. If many chose Mirror — congregation might be in conviction season. If Food — they're hungry; check spiritual feeding diet broadly.
3Sa S.O.A.P.S in-group try — sinong pax ang naka-fill ng all 5 boxes? Sinong stuck sa S box (Scripture) lang?
All-5 = ready disciples; ipagdiwang ngayong linggo. Stuck-on-S = may struggle sa Bible literacy; pair with Anchor mentor.
4Sa Sharing (last) box — ilan ang nagsulat ng aktwal na EOLO name?
Ito ang real metric. Kung mababa, ang EOLO list ng pax ay theoretical lang — may follow-up work bago L3.
5Sino sa pax ang humingi ng help ngayong session? Sa anong specific area?
Don't extrapolate. Just name. Pastor decides follow-up.
For the Interns
6Anong tanong ang sinabi sa'yo ng pax sa table mo na hindi mo agad nasagot?
No shame. Naming a question = growth. Pastor / facilitator addresses it next round.
7Sa table mo, anong klase ng lupa ang pinakamadalas napili? May surprise?
Patterns by table. Some tables cluster Mabato (sa hirap nila tumatalikod). Iba Matinik (busy professionals). Note.
8Kung mag-fa-facilitate ka ng L2 sa LCG niyo next month, anong gagawin mong iba?
This question is what makes interns into leaders. Push for specificity. Not "magiging mas magaling."
For Everybody (Pastoral Closing)
9Sino ang isang pax na nakita natin ngayon — na hindi natin nakita dati?
The point of debrief is not assessment. It's seeing people. Walang Pharisaic check-list — pure shepherd attention.
10Anong isang specific commitment ang gagawin ng team this week as a result of what we noticed today?
One. Specific. Doable in 7 days. Hindi "mas magiging maingat tayo."
🏠 LCG Weekly Mode
BTLI runs bi-monthly. LCG meets weekly. This pane is for the in-between weeks (Week 2 and Week 4 after L2 is taught). The goal: reinforce without re-teaching. Same 5-segment, ~40-minute agenda. Different content per week.
5-Segment Weekly Agenda
1
KAMUSTAHAN + Memory Verse
~5 min
Quick check-ins — kumusta na? Then together recite Awit 1:2 from memory. If someone forgot, no shame — recite together as a group. The memory verse anchors every weekly LCG meeting for the duration of L2's cycle.
2
PAGDIRIWANG · Celebration
~7 min
S.O.A.P.S notebook show. Each member shows their filled-in S.O.A.P.S — even just one entry. Don't read it aloud unless they want to. The point is visible faithfulness. Notice who has a notebook. Notice who is filling all 5 boxes (especially Sharing). Acknowledge by name.
3
PAGTUTULONG · Peer Help
~12 min
Not facilitator advice. Peer help. One member shares a current Application from their S.O.A.P.S — something they're trying to live out from their reading. The group responds: "Anong specific way puwede ka naming matulungan ngayong linggo para magawa mo iyan?" The facilitator only steps in if the group is stuck — and even then, redirects: "Ano sa palagay niyo?"
4
EOLO PULSE
~10 min
One update per member on one EOLO name. Pegging it to L2: "Sa S.O.A.P.S mo ngayong linggo, may na-share ka ba sa kanya? Anong response niya?" If they say "wala pa" — no shame. The question itself is the prompt to do it. Document who shared what — pastoral follow-up data.
5
PANALANGIN PARA SA ISA'T ISA
~5 min
Pairs or trios. Each prays for the other's specific application from segment 3 and their EOLO person from segment 4. Specific prayer — hindi generic. "Lord, tulungan Mo si [name] na magawa niya itong [specific thing] sa Biyernes pagdating niya sa bahay."
Week 2 vs Week 4 — Variation
📅 WEEK 2 — Just after L2
Segment 2 emphasis: Celebrate starting the 30-day challenge. "Sino pumili ng Bible book?" "Ilang chapters na?" Adrenalin pa ng start.

Segment 3 emphasis: Help with logistics — "Anong oras ka magde-devotion? Saan?" Structural Ability source.

Segment 4 emphasis: First-share attempts. "Sinabi mo na ba kahit isa kahit kanino?"
📅 WEEK 4 — Just before L3
Segment 2 emphasis: Celebrate persisting. Adrenalin gone. Faithfulness mode na. "Sino dumaan sa Day 14 na low?"

Segment 3 emphasis: Help with discouragement — "Anong nawawala ka sa enthusiasm?" Personal Motivation source.

Segment 4 emphasis: Deepening conversations. "Nag-ano kayo? Anong sumunod?"
24-Hour Follow-up Message Template
Hi [Name], salamat sa LCG kagabi. Ipinagdarasal kita ngayong linggo para sa specific application mo — [restate from segment 3] — at para kay [EOLO name]. Bago mag-Sunday, padalhan mo ako ng isang short text — anong sinasabi sa'yo ni Lord sa S.O.A.P.S mo ngayong linggo? 🙏
Six Sources of Influence — How Each Segment Engages
  • Segment 1 (Memory verse): Personal Ability (scaffolding recall) + Social Motivation (recited together).
  • Segment 2 (Celebration): Social Motivation (peer affirmation) + Personal Motivation (link discipline to identity).
  • Segment 3 (Peer Help): Social Ability (find strength in numbers) + Structural Ability (concrete help offers).
  • Segment 4 (EOLO Pulse): Personal Motivation (connect to values — "I love this person") + Social Motivation (accountability).
  • Segment 5 (Prayer): Personal Motivation (spiritual connection) + Social Motivation (relational care).
⚠️ Prototype Note
The LCG Weekly mode in this file mirrors the L1 prototype structure. Do not propagate to L3+ until validated with real LC leaders for at least one Month-1 cycle (L1+L2 combined). Pastor will signal when to roll out the pattern broadly.

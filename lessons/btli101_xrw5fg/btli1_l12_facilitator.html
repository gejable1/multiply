Lesson DNA
The Big Idea — Read This First
5 min · Before opening any slide
▼

The session in one sentence: Nag-iisa lang ang kagaya mo sa mundo — comparison steals the mission God designed you for; self-control (Mac Lake's Leading Yourself Module 2) is the wall that keeps the comparison loop out, and a disciple who discovers their S.H.A.P.E. (Spiritual Gifts · Heart · Abilities · Personality · Experiences) stops living in someone else's shadow and starts serving from God's design.

What changes inside a participant by the end of 90 minutes:

  • They see: The comparison trap — the crow who interviewed the swan, the parrot, and the peacock, and discovered that everyone was envying someone else. Comparison is a prison with no door.
  • They understand: Ephesians 2:10 — poiema — they are God's masterpiece, designed before birth for good works He prepared in advance (Jeremiah 1:5, Galatians 1:15-16). Proverbs 25:28 — without self-control, the city walls are broken and comparison walks right in. Mac Lake's Emotional Intelligence Triangle (God · You · Others) frames the honest self-look, and the S.H.A.P.E. framework is five windows into that design.
  • They do: Complete a first-pass S.H.A.P.E. inventory, assemble a one-paragraph Self-Portrait, declare their own Eric Liddell sentence out loud, and carry home two assignments: the MULTIPLY assessment battery and Leading Yourself's 10-day daily emotions journal.
🎯 The One Movement That Matters Most
GAWIN. If a TALAKAY debate runs short — fine. If DALHIN gets compressed — fine. But they must leave with a written first-pass S.H.A.P.E. inventory and their Liddell declaration filled in. That is the artifact this lesson produces. Everything else serves it.
Source Acknowledgment

This lesson adapts UNLAD Lesson 2 (Understanding My Unique Design) — the crow fable, 2 Corinthians 10:12, the poiema anchor, the S.H.A.P.E. walkthrough, and the Eric Liddell TUGON — with the S.H.A.P.E. framework popularized by Rick Warren (with Erik Rees' S.H.A.P.E.). By the pipeline's positional pairing, this lesson also carries Mac Lake's Leading Yourself Module 2 (Emotional Intelligence · Self-Control) — Proverbs 25:28, the Emotional Intelligence Triangle (God · You · Others), the five EI proficiencies as the A-E snapshot, and the daily emotions journal as homework. The competence layer is native to MULTIPLY: the platform's own assessment battery (Spiritual Gifts, Strengths, DISC, Enneagram, Love Language) turns the book's one-time worksheet into a living self-portrait each disciple carries in the Member Tool.

🌿 Character Anchor — Self-Control (Mac Lake LY Module 2)
Why Self-Control? Mac Lake defines it as managing and restraining your impulses, emotions, and desires — exercising discipline to make thoughtful decisions. The comparison loop is an emotions problem: inggit, insecurity, at pride ang pumapasok kapag sira ang pader (Proverbs 25:28). A disciple who calls God's workmanship in themselves "walang silbi" has let an unexamined emotion contradict the Maker's verdict — gently correct it with Ephesians 2:10. Emotional Intelligence — understanding your own or others' emotions and allowing the Holy Spirit to guide you toward a wise response — is the skill this character grows into.

🎯 Intern Pre-Session Briefing — 15 min before (Cohort only)
Gather all interns. Cover: (1) The main truth of today — you are God's poiema, comparison is the thief, S.H.A.P.E. is the map. (2) The fable is a performance, not a reading — assign the 4 reader roles and let them rehearse the voices once. (3) The two pastoral moments not to rush — the Experiences window in GAWIN (painful stories may surface; nobody is forced to share what they wrote) and the Interact debates (both extremes — inferiority and superiority — are real people at your tables; debate ideas, never persons). (4) During the GAWIN writing block, interns are a quiet presence: help stuck people with prompts, never with answers. Pray together. Send them to their tables.

Movement 1 · PUKAW
Engage — The Comparison Trap
12 minutes · Session opens here
▼
Callback — The Lesson 11 Journal (Slide 2)

Before anything new: honor what they carried home. Lesson 11 ended with a 10-day mission journal. Ask: "Sino ang naka-check kahit isang araw sa 10-day tracker natin? Itaas ang kamay." Celebrate every raised hand. Ask ONE volunteer for a 30-second story: "Anong isang bagay ang ginawa mo alinsunod sa misyon mo?"

🎯 Celebrate, Don't Shame
Someone with zero checks is watching your face right now. Say it plainly: "Kung wala kang na-check — welcome pa rin. Ang tracker ay tulong, hindi timbangan. Ngayong gabi may bago tayong makikita." The dormant journal becomes a conversation starter for their discipler, never a verdict.
Memory Verse — Ephesians 2:10 (Slide 3)

Read it together, twice — once from the slide, once with eyes closed repeating after you. Land on one word: "pinakamahusay na gawa" — masterpiece. Tell them: "Babalikan natin ang salitang ito mamaya. Greek pa nga ang pinanggalingan e." Plant the seed; don't teach it yet.

The Hook — Ang Uwak na Naghanap ng Pinakamasayang Ibon (Slide 4)

Tell the UNLAD fable — theatrically. A crow flies free and happy, until it sees the swan: "Ikaw na siguro ang pinakamasayang ibon — sobrang ganda ng puti mong balahibo!" The swan sighs: "Hindi e. Nang makita ko ang two-tone na balahibo ni parrot, nalungkot ako." The parrot: "Masaya ako... noon... bago ko makita si peacock! Boring ang kulay ko sa tabi niya." The crow flies to the peacock — surrounded by fans taking pictures. Surely THIS is the happiest bird? The peacock sighs the longest sigh: "Maganda nga, nakakulong naman. Maraming fans, walang privacy. Siguro magiging masaya ako kung kasing-laya mo na lang ako. Sana isa na lang akong uwak!"

🎯 The Setup — Why This Hook
The circle closes on itself: the crow envies the swan envies the parrot envies the peacock envies the crow. Nobody in the comparison loop is happy — because the loop has no exit. You are not teaching birds. You are holding up a mirror. Resist explaining the moral — Slide 5's question does that work.
Cohort Activity 5 TABLES
⚡ Reader's Theater — Apat na Ibon
4 Volunteers × 1 Narrator (You) (5 min)
Setup (pre-arranged): Your 4 pre-asked volunteers stand at their tables as uwak, swan, parrot, peacock. You narrate; they deliver their one line each — with feelings. Encourage hirit and hugot voices.

The final line lands (30 sec): After "Sana isa na lang akong uwak!" — pause. Full stop. Let the room feel the loop close.

Then Slide 5: Tables discuss the two questions and each participant writes their own answers in the Participant Guide.
Small Group Activity 1 TABLE
⚡ Round-the-Table Fable
Shared Reading, Shared Mirror (5 min)
Read (3 min): Assign the 4 bird voices around the circle; you narrate. Keep it light — laughter opens hearts.

Land (2 min): Ask the Slide 5 questions conversationally, then everyone writes their own answers in the Participant Guide before anyone shares.
1-on-1 Conversation MENTOR-MENTEE
⚡ Story + Silence
Tell · Ask · Wait (8 min)
Tell (3 min): Tell the fable casually, like tsismis about four birds. End on the peacock's line and stop.

Ask (2 min): "Ano sa tingin mo ang moral?" Then the heavier one: "Kanino ka madalas magkumpara ng sarili mo?"

Wait (3 min): The first honest answer usually comes after an uncomfortable pause. Don't rescue. Then both of you write in the Participant Guide.

Movement 2 · TUKLAS
Discover — Masterpiece by Design
20 minutes · Guide discovery, do not lecture
▼
The Big Idea (Slide 6)

Project it and read it slowly, twice: "Nag-iisa lang ang kagaya mo sa mundo — iwasan ang kakakumpara. Mabuhay at maglingkod nang husto ayon sa disenyo ng Lumikha." This single sentence is the spine of the night. Everything after it is evidence and application.

Scripture 1 — 2 Corinthians 10:12 (Slide 7)

Have someone read the Pinoy Version aloud — it bites: "Ginagawa nilang standard ang mga sarili nila, tapos sila-sila lang din ang nagko-compare sa isat-isa! Mga 'di sila nag-iisip!" Point at the last phrase: Apostle Paul calls the comparison game foolishness. Not a weakness. Not a quirk. Kamangmangan.

Connect back to the birds: "Ang uwak, swan, parrot, at peacock — lahat sila ginawang standard ang isa't isa. Lahat sila malungkot. Mga 'di sila nag-iisip."

Scripture 2 — Ephesians 2:10, POIEMA (Slide 8)

Now the exit from the prison. Read the NLT: "For we are God's masterpiece." Teach the one Greek word of the night: poiema — literally work of art. Same root as our word "poem." In some translations: workmanship.

Land the logic: a painting does not compare itself to another painting — it fulfills the painter's intent. "Hindi na natin kailangang magkumpara. Kailangan lang nating unawain at lakaran ang plano Niya."

🎯 Watch the Room Here
For participants who grew up being told they were worthless — walang silbi, walang kwenta — this verse is not information, it is surgery. If you see wet eyes at Slide 8, slow down. Let the verse do its work. The question slide that follows (Slide 9) gives them a private place to respond.
Question — Facing the Maestro (Slide 9)

Participant Guide question, written privately first: "Kung haharap ka sa ating Dakilang Maestro ngayon, masasabi mo ba nang may kumpiyansa na nabubuhay ka ayon sa Kaniyang disenyo?" Then 1-2 volunteers share — volunteers only.

Natatanging Disenyo, Natatanging Ambag (Slide 10)

"Hindi ka ginawang masterpiece ni God para maging display lang!" A masterpiece with a mission: the good works were prepared in advance (Eph 2:10b). Show the two witnesses — Jeremiah 1:5 ("Bago ka pa ipinaglihi... pinili na kita") and Galatians 1:15-16 (Paul: "pinili niya ako bago pa ako ipanganak"). God's pattern: design precedes birth; mission is stitched into design.

Question — Your Mission (Slide 11)

Participant Guide: "Alam mo na ba kung ano ang iyong misyon sa buhay? Ano ito?" Lesson 11 alumni should pull up their mission statement draft — this is continuity, not a new question. First-timers write their best current guess.

The Map — S.H.A.P.E. (Slide 12)

Bridge: "Kung clueless ka pa sa specific na misyon mo — may mapa. Limang bintana para makita ang disenyo ng Dios sa'yo." Introduce the acronym popularized by Rick Warren: Spiritual Gifts · Heart · Abilities · Personality · Experiences. Quote Erik Rees: when your dreams align with God's desires, you gain the confidence to accept your unique kingdom assignment.

Tell them the plan: "Pag-aaralan natin ang limang bintana ngayon (TALAKAY), tapos sasagutan ninyo ito nang personal mamaya (GAWIN). Hindi ito personality quiz lang — ito ang mapa papunta sa ministry placement ninyo."

Movement 3 · TALAKAY
Deepen — Self-Control, the Triangle, Five Windows, Two Debates
20 minutes · Teaching + Interact
▼
Character Anchor — Self-Control · Proverbs 25:28 (Slide 13)

Open TALAKAY with the wall, not the windows. Read Proverbs 25:28: "Like a city whose walls are broken through is a person who lacks self-control." Mac Lake's definition: managing and restraining your impulses, emotions, and desires — exercising discipline to make thoughtful decisions. Then land the connection: ang comparison loop ay problema ng emosyon. The crow didn't have an information problem — it had an unguarded heart. Self-control is the wall that keeps inggit out and design in.

Question — Pressure & Conflict (Slide 14)

Participant Guide question from Leading Yourself: "Paano ka karaniwang nagre-react — sa labas at sa loob — kapag may pressure o conflict? Paano ito mababago ng self-control?" Push for a real situation, hindi generic. Written privately; no sharing required — this seeds the emotions journal assignment in DALHIN.

The Emotional Intelligence Triangle (Slide 15)

Draw it or project it: God at the apex, You and Others at the base. Emotional Intelligence, per Mac Lake: understanding your own or others' emotions and allowing the Holy Spirit to guide you toward a wise response. Four movements around the triangle: awareness (pangalanan ang nararamdaman), honesty (pangalanan ang tunay na ugat, hindi ang justified version), empathy (unawain ang pananaw ng iba), wisdom (tumugon ayon sa katotohanan ng Dios). Why here: the five S.H.A.P.E. windows require exactly this honesty — hindi makikita ang disenyo sa pamamagitan ng hindi nasusuring emosyon.

Window 1 — S · Spiritual Gifts (Slide 16)

Not the same as natural talent. Quote Leslie Flynn: "May kinalaman ang talents sa techniques at methods; ang spiritual gifts naman ay may kinalaman sa spiritual abilities. Nakadepende ang talent sa sariling galing, nakadepende ang spiritual gifts sa kakayanan ng Dios." Three Biblical truths, fast: (1) It is not about you or for you — 1 Cor 12:7, "para sa ikabubuti ng lahat." (2) Every believer has received at least one — 1 Cor 7:7a. (3) We are not all gifted alike — same verse.

MULTIPLY note: "May Spiritual Gifts Assessment tayo sa Member Tool — babalikan natin iyan mamaya sa GAWIN."

Window 2 — H · Heart (Slide 17)

Rick Warren: just as each of us has unique thumbprints, eye prints, and voice prints, we each have a unique emotional heartbeat — it races for the specific things God wired us to care about. Preview the three Passion Questions they will answer in GAWIN: Ano ang nagpapa-alab sa puso mo? Kanino ka sobrang concerned? Anong God-centered dreams ang nais mong matupad? Nescafe hook for #2: "Para kanino ka bumabangon?"

Window 3 — A · Abilities (Slide 18)

Tom Paterson: a person who never discovers or develops their God-given abilities lives "salat sa layunin, kahulugan at halaga" — poor in purpose, meaning, and worth. Walang tapon at walang sayang sa mga abilidad na ibinigay ni Lord. Clarify the comfort-zone caveat: God WILL sometimes call them outside their strengths — the bottom line is dependence on Him, not self-optimization.

Window 4 — P · Personality (Slide 19)

APA definition, simplified: your distinct way of thinking, relating, and behaving. The punchline the room needs: "Walang mas superior sa mga categories na yan." Introvert is not broken extrovert. Reserved is not failed expressive. God gave your innate personality so your gifts, abilities, and heart get expressed effectively — and our differences exist so we can complement one another.

Window 5 — E · Experiences (Slide 20)

The window people flinch at. Successes AND wounds. Read 2 Corinthians 1:4 — God comforts us in all our troubles so that we can comfort others. The book names it directly: abuse, poverty, addiction, serious illness — these histories, surrendered whole to God, become a kakaibang attraction: credibility no seminary can grant, for reaching people walking the same road.

🎯 Pastoral Caution — Handle Experiences With Both Hands
Say it out loud before GAWIN: "Ang isusulat ninyo sa Experiences window ay sa inyo at sa Dios. Walang pipilitin mag-share." If someone discloses abuse or trauma to you afterward, listen fully, thank them for trusting you, and connect them with the Pastor privately. Never turn a fresh wound into a public testimony on the spot.
Interact Debate 1 (Slide 21)

"Justifiable ba ang inferiority complex ng ibang Christians dahil wala sila ng mga bagay na taglay ng iba?" Let the room argue both sides for a few minutes, then land it: sympathy for the wound, but the verdict is Ephesians 2:10 — calling God's masterpiece worthless is not humility, it is disagreement with the Artist.

Interact Debate 2 (Slide 22)

"Paano ka magre-respond sa isang believer na masyado namang mataas ang tingin sa sarili dahil sa mga S.H.A.P.E. na taglay niya?" Draw out the mirror-image insight: inferiority and superiority are the same disease — eyes locked on self instead of the Designer, an unguarded city on both counts (Prov 25:28). 1 Cor 12:7 is the cure for both: the gifts are "para sa ikabubuti ng lahat," never trophies.

Cohort Format 5 TABLES
💡 Table Debates — Agree Corner, Disagree Corner
Two Rounds × 3 Minutes (6 min)
Round 1 (Slide 21): Each table takes 90 seconds to argue BOTH sides among themselves, then plants one sticky note on the wall: AGREE or DISAGREE + one sentence why. You read 2-3 aloud.

Round 2 (Slide 22): Same rhythm. Then your 60-second landing: same disease, same cure.

Everyone writes their personal answers in the Participant Guide as the debate closes.
Small Group Format 1 TABLE
💡 Circle Debate
Everyone Speaks Once (6 min)
Question 1: go around the circle — one sentence each, agree or disagree with a reason. Question 2: open floor. You land both with the same-disease insight. Writing follows in the Participant Guide.
1-on-1 Conversation MENTOR-MENTEE
💡 The Honest Question
Which Side Do You Lean? (6 min)
After both questions, ask the personal version gently: "Sa dalawang ito — pagkamaliit o pagkataas ng tingin sa sarili — saan ka mas madalas madulas?" Share your own honest answer FIRST. Mentee mirrors. Then both write.

Movement 4 · GAWIN
Practice — The S.H.A.P.E. Inventory
20 minutes · The Artifact-Producing Movement
▼
The Workflow (Slide 23)

Everyone opens the Participant Guide on their phone (QR on screen). The next slides walk the room through the inventory one question at a time — you advance, they write. Quiet worship music under the whole block helps. Interns roam as quiet presence: prompts for the stuck, never answers.

S — Gifts Recall (Slide 24)

"Balikan ang iyong Spiritual Gifts Assessment sa MULTIPLY. Anong gifts ang ibinigay ni Lord sa'yo — at paano mo nagagamit ang mga ito sa ministry ngayon?" If they have never taken it, they write their best guess and star it — the assessment battery in DALHIN closes the gap.

H — Three Passion Questions (Slides 25-27)

One question per slide, ~2 minutes each: the one thing they would do free-of-charge for life; the people group closest to their heart; the God-centered dream they want fulfilled in their lifetime (like the boy's five loaves and two fish, John 6:9).

A — Abilities Inventory (Slide 28)

Four rapid prompts on one slide, answered as one block: strengths · what they deeply enjoy · where others commend them · what is hard for others but natural for them.

P — Personality Pairs (Slide 29)

Six taps in the Participant Guide: mag-isa/maraming kasama · expressive/reserved · maingay/tahimik · cooperative/competitive · risk-taker/safe-player · leader/follower. Repeat the line as they tap: "Walang mas superior sa mga categories na ito."

E — Experiences (Slide 30)

The gentlest two minutes of the night. One success God has already used; one wound God could use (2 Cor 1:4). Restate the privacy line from TALAKAY. Nobody shares this block aloud.

The Self-Portrait (Slide 31)

Five one-line summaries — S, H, A, P, E — and the Participant Guide assembles them into a single paragraph: their first-draft S.H.A.P.E. Self-Portrait. Imperfect is the goal. First drafts only.

Eric Liddell (Slide 32)

Tell it in 60 seconds: two world-class runners at the 1924 Paris Olympics — Harold Abrahams ran to win; Eric Liddell ran for the glory of Christ, refused to run on Sunday, won the 400m with a world record, and later gave his life as a missionary to China. The line the film Chariots of Fire made immortal: "Nilikha ako ng Dios na matulin, at sa tuwing tumatakbo ako, nararamdaman ko ang Kaniyang kasiyahan."

The Declaration (Slide 33)

Fill in the blanks in the Participant Guide: "Nilikha ako ng Dios na ___, at sa tuwing ___ ako, nararamdaman ko ang Kaniyang kasiyahan." Then — pairs. Each person says their declaration out loud to their seatmate. Something changes when design is spoken, not just written.

🎯 If Time Collapses
Protect Slides 24, 28, and 33 — gifts, abilities, declaration. The Heart questions can compress to one; the Portrait can be finished at home. The declaration spoken aloud is the emotional summit of the night — do not cut it.

Send — Formation Continues at Home
8 minutes · Assignment + Verse + Pangako + Closing
▼
Takdang-Aralin — Two Assignments (Slide 34)

Assignment 1 — the assessment battery. The book says "HUWAG NA HUWAG ITONG I-MISS" about its SHAPE test — ours lives in the app. Before Lesson 13, complete every MULTIPLY assessment not yet taken: Spiritual Gifts · Strengths · DISC · Enneagram · Love Language. Path: Member Tool → My Profile → My Assessments. The Participant Guide has the 5-item checklist. "Dalhin ang mga resulta sa Lesson 13 — magagamit natin sila sa servant leadership."

Assignment 2 — the daily emotions journal (Leading Yourself Module 2). Each night, a few minutes: anong mga emosyon ang naramdaman ko ngayong araw, paano ako tumugon, napamahalaan ko ba nang maayos? The Participant Guide has a 10-day tracker — it restores the L11 rhythm and gives Lesson 13 its callback. Point the deep-divers to the guide's at-home section: the Being Honest / Showing Empathy / Acting Wisely reflections, the downloadable EI Triangle tool at multiplygroup.org/leadingyourself, and the Ignatian Examen.

🎯 Why This Matters Beyond Homework
Completed assessments feed ministry placement — the disciple's gifts profile meets the church's ministry needs. This is the lesson where the paper framework becomes platform reality. Follow up with disciplers this week: who needs help logging in?
EOLO + Pangako (Slide 35)

EOLO bridge: "Hindi ka ginawang masterpiece para maging display. Isulat ang pangalan ng iyong EOLO person — at kung paano mo magagamit ang iyong S.H.A.P.E. para mahalin siya ngayong linggo." Then the Pangako: "Sa susunod na dalawang linggo, isasabuhay ko ang disenyo ng Dios sa akin sa pamamagitan ng ___." One thing. Specific. Doable.

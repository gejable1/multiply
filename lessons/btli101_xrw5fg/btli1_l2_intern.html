🎯 Para sa'yo, kapatid intern
This is your second BTLI lesson as an intern. Last lesson (L1 — Prayer), you sat at your table and modeled praying with the ACTS journal. This lesson, you do something similar — but watch closely. Bible meditation is harder to model than prayer. People can pray without thinking. People cannot S.O.A.P.S without thinking. The friction in this lesson is a feature, not a bug. Your job is to sit alongside the friction — not remove it.
1
Pre-Session Briefing
15 min before participants arrive
▼
Show Up With This Done
  • Read the participant booklet pages on Bible Meditation before arriving.
  • Personally fill out a S.O.A.P.S row last night using any verse — you cannot facilitate something you haven't done.
  • Prayed for your table (by name, if you have last session's roster).
  • Bring your own Bible + a notebook + a pen.
🎯 The skill upgrade from L1 → L2
L1 you modeled ACTS prayer. L2 you model silent thinking on a verse + writing. The hard part is that you cannot perform thinking. You have to actually do it. Sit. Read. Pause. Write. The pax will copy you.
What the Lead Facilitator Will Cover
Faithfulness as the L1+L2 unified anchor. Forrest Lunsway story. 5 roles of the Bible. 4 soils. S.O.A.P.S in-group try on Filipos 4:6-7. 30-day challenge. Memory verse: Awit 1:2.
Your three jobs today: (1) Model — physically do the S.O.A.P.S yourself at the table. (2) Watch — notice who is stuck, who is fast, who is silent. (3) Care — soft prompts only; no lecturing.
2
PUKAW · Prayer-Pact Callback (8 min)
First chance to observe carryover from L1
▼
🌱 New skill for you
Listening for L1 carryover. When the lead facilitator asks "Kumusta ang prayer pact?", watch how many in your table physically lean in vs. avoid eye contact. Iyon ang pastoral data. Hindi para i-confront — para i-care.
Observation Checklist
  • Sino sa table ko ang nag-share tungkol sa prayer pact, even briefly?
  • Sino ang umiwas ng tingin? (Not shaming — possibly humble.)
  • May nag-shame ba sa sarili? ("Hindi ko nagawa, sorry...") — soft response: "Salamat sa honesty mo. Iniinvite ka pa rin para mag-start ngayong linggo."
  • Sino ang naging energizer sa table sa unang segment?
📝 Sulat dito ng mabilis (in your phone or notebook) — pangalan + one-word state ng bawat isa sa table mo nang matapos ang PUKAW.
⚠️ Avoid this
Do not call out the people who didn't pray last week. The prayer-pact callback is a celebration moment, not an audit moment. Lead facilitator handles the framing. You hold the room's safety.
3
TUKLAS · 5 Roles + Reflection (15 min)
Watch which role each pax picks
▼
🎯 The single most useful thing you can do
When the facilitator gives the 90-second silent reflection ("Ano ang pinakakailangan mo sa 5 roles?"), quietly check what each pax in your table circles or writes. Don't read intrusively — just glance. The pattern by table is pastoral data.
5 Roles — What Each Pattern Often Means
1. KATOTOHANAN Pax in spiritual confusion. Often new believers or people coming out of false teaching.

2. SPIRITUAL FOOD Hungry pax. Often newer believers or those returning from a dry season.

3. SANDATA/SWORD Pax in active spiritual battle. Temptation, conflict, depression. Don't ignore this signal.

4. SALAMIN Pax with conviction — already knows where they're falling short. Quiet seasoned believer often.

5. TANGLAW Pax in decision-making season. Career, relationship, ministry. Looking for direction.
During the 90s Silence — Your Posture
  • Lower your eyes. Don't scan the room. Honor the silence.
  • If a pax looks lost — slide your booklet toward them so they see your underline. Don't whisper.
  • If someone laughs nervously — small smile, then return to silence. Don't engage.

4
TALAKAY · 4 Soils Discussion (12 min)
7 min table discussion — your facilitation peak
▼
🌱 New skill for you
Holding silence after a question. When you ask "Anong klaseng lupa ang puso mo?" — count silently to 7 before saying anything else. Most interns rescue silence too fast. The silence is where the answer comes from.
3 Questions in Order
  1. Anong klaseng lupa ang puso mo ngayon? Bakit? (Most personal — start here.)
  2. Ano ang pangunahing dahilan kung bakit minsan hindi nakakapag-devotion ang isang believer? (Move from personal to general — less threatening.)
  3. Sang-ayon ka ba kay A.W. Tozer? (End with a sharpened question — gives them something to chew on.)
Soft Prompts When Stuck
  • If no one talks: "Walang correct answer. Honesto lang." Wait 5 more seconds.
  • If one person dominates: gently redirect: "Salamat. Sino pa? Si [name], anong sa'yo?"
  • If someone says "wala akong masagot": "Pwedeng pakinggan mo muna ang iba?"
  • If a pax shares a serious struggle (e.g., "hindi ako nag-de-devotion dahil galit ako sa Dios"): Don't fix. "Salamat sa pagbahagi. Ipagdarasal kita." Note name for pastor follow-up.
⚠️ The trap to avoid
Do not interpret the 4 soils as "Mataba is best, Tabing-Kalsada is worst." All 4 are responses to the same Word. Iba-iba ang context. Diagnostic, not judgmental. Saan ka ngayon? Hindi saan ka dapat.
5
GAWIN · S.O.A.P.S In-Group Try (12 min)
Your most hands-on segment
▼
⭐ The single most important thing today
When the facilitator hands out the 5-box S.O.A.P.S sheet, fill your own out alongside the pax. Visibly. Slowly. So they see how you read Filipos 4:6-7, pause, then write. Modeling thinking is harder than modeling action. But pax will mimic you.
The 5 Boxes — What to Watch For Per Box
S — Scripture Some pax will struggle to pick what to write. Soft prompt: "Anong verse natigil ka? Iyan."
O — Observation Pax may rush to interpretation. Redirect: "Hindi pa kung ano ang ibig sabihin. Sino lang ang nagsasalita? Kanino? Saan?"
A — Application Pax often write generic ("magiging mas matiyaga ako"). Push for specific: "Saan? Kanino? Kailan ngayong linggo?"
P — Prayer Easy box for most. If someone is stuck, suggest 1 line: "Lord, [response to what I read]."
S — Sharing The hardest box. Pax will look up confused. Remind: "Tingnan mo ang EOLO list mo. Sinong puwedeng makinabang sa natutunan mo?" Acceptable answer: a name. Better answer: a name + a "when."
Observation Checklist During GAWIN
  • Sinong pax sa table ko ang naka-fill ng all 5 boxes?
  • Sinong stuck sa S box (Scripture) — i.e., struggling with Bible literacy?
  • Sinong nag-skip ng Sharing box?
  • Sinong nag-write ng actual EOLO name sa Sharing box?

6
DALHIN · 30-Day Challenge + Send-Off (8 min)
Set them up for the gap before L3
▼
Your Role in DALHIN
Quiet. The lead facilitator carries this segment. Your job: after the closing prayer, before pax leave, walk over to the 1-2 people in your table you flagged during TALAKAY or GAWIN as needing follow-up. Don't lecture. One sentence:
🎤 Sample one-liner
"Kapatid, pinapanalangin kita ngayong linggo. Tetextan kita Wednesday para mag-check kung anong chapter ka na ng [Bible book niya]."
Observation Checklist for DALHIN
  • Sinong pax sa table ko ang aktwal na nakapili na ng Bible book?
  • Sinong nag-commit ng oras (umaga, gabi)?
  • Sinong umalis ng walang notebook? (Possible follow-up: ask if they need one.)

Observation, table-care, and debrief prep for interns
📖 Roma 12:1 🌿 Joy ⚡ 3 Sangkap + Signed Paper
100%
🎯 Para sa'yo, kapatid intern
This is your third BTLI lesson as an intern. L1 you modeled praying. L2 you modeled silent thinking. L3 is heavier. Pagsuko is not a discipline lesson — it is a posture lesson. The signed-paper ritual is the emotional climax of the entire first month of BTLI. Your work this session is not to teach. It is to sit reverently alongside the Spirit's work in your pax.

You will see people weep. You will see people resist. Both are data. Neither is yours to fix.
1
Pre-Session Briefing
15 min before participants arrive
▼
Show Up With This Done
  • Read the participant booklet pages on Pagsuko before arriving.
  • Personally fill out your own 3-Sangkap Inventory last night. If you cannot name Sangkap 2 honestly for yourself, you cannot help anyone else do so.
  • Pray over the list of names at your table.
  • Bring your own Bible + notebook + pen.
🌿 Character anchor — Joy (not Self-Control)
L3's character is Joy, revised from the skeleton. This matters for your posture in the room: surrender is responsive love grounded in mercy received — not gritted-teeth self-restraint. If a pax frames their surrender as "sige na lang, sumusuko na ako para hindi na kasalanan," redirect: "Anong mercy ng Dios ang naranasan mo na nagbubunga ngayong yielding na ito?" Start from gratitude, not duty.
What the Lead Facilitator Will Cover
S.O.A.P.S callback (L2). Mokusatsu story → unconditional surrender. Consecration definition. 1 Cor 6:19-20 + Roma 14:7-8. Pagsuko vs Pagsunod (Lucas 6:46-47). Moody quote. The 3-Sangkap Inventory built on Romans 12:1. The Signed-Paper Ritual. "I Surrender All" hymn closing. Memory verse: Roma 12:1.
Your three jobs today: (1) Model — physically fill your own 3-Sangkap at the table. (2) Witness — notice patterns of honesty, evasion, and breakthrough. (3) Hold space — especially during the signed-paper ritual; reverence over rescue.
2
PUKAW · S.O.A.P.S Callback + Mokusatsu (8 min)
Carrying gravity into the room
▼
🌱 New skill for you
Holding gravity without graphic detail. The Mokusatsu story ends in 200,000 dead. You will be tempted to soften it with humor or to dwell in it with morbid detail. Resist both. Hold the weight. Stay still. Trust the silence.
Observation Checklist · S.O.A.P.S Callback
  • Sino sa table ko ang aktwal na nakapag-S.O.A.P.S kahit ilang araw?
  • Sino ang nag-pick ng John (the recommended book)? May iba ba na nag-pick ng ibang book?
  • Sino ang nag-share ng natutunan sa SOAPS na pumipigil sa kanila o nag-bunga ng pagbabago?
  • Sino ang tahimik o iniiwasan ang tanong? (Pastoral data — soft follow-up needed.)
During the Mokusatsu Story — Your Posture
⚠️ Gravity-handling for interns
When the lead facilitator says "Hiroshima. Nagasaki. 200,000+ dead" — look down or close your eyes briefly. Do NOT scan the room. Do NOT exchange glances with another intern. Honor the silence with your body. The pax will read your stillness as permission to feel the weight too.

When the facilitator pivots — "Paano kaya sa relasyon natin sa Dios?" — look up. Resume engagement. The gravity has done its work.

3
TUKLAS · Consecration + Pagsunod vs Pagsuway (15 min)
Watch for the "I'm working on it" deflection
▼
🎯 The single most useful thing you can do
When the facilitator asks "Alin ka — sumusunod o sumusuway?" — do not let your table answer collectively. The question is meant to land as personal. If someone says "working on it," gently redirect: "Working on it pa rin ay sumusuway or sumusunod nang hindi perpekto. May difference." The disciple sumusunod imperfectly. The non-disciple sumusuway and calls it humility.
5 Roles → 3 Sangkap Bridge — Watch For
SURRENDER-AS-DEFEAT Pax who shrink when "unconditional surrender" is spoken — body language closes. Often raised in homes where authority meant abuse. Soft prompt: "Hindi forced. Responsive love kay Lord."

SURRENDER-AS-PERFORMANCE Pax who nod enthusiastically too fast. They surrendered abstractly long ago and never tested it. Watch them at Sangkap 2 — that's where they'll get stuck.

SURRENDER-AS-NEGOTIATION Pax who try to add conditions ("Yes, but..."). Note. Do not confront. Pastor-level follow-up needed.

SURRENDER-AS-RELIEF Pax who breathe deeper, body softens when "consecration" is defined. They've been carrying something. Celebrate quietly with eye contact + small nod.
During Pagsunod/Pagsuway — Your Posture
  • Don't laugh nervously at the sharp binary. Hold the question.
  • If a pax visibly squirms — leave them alone. The Spirit is doing the work.
  • If a pax tries to argue the binary ("it's a spectrum"), gently: "That's the world's framing. Anong sabi ni Lord?" Don't debate further.

4
TALAKAY · World's View + Moody (12 min)
8 min table discussion — your facilitation peak
▼
🌱 New skill for you
Letting the Moody challenge sting. When you ask "Naniniwala ka ba kay Moody — mas marami Siyang magagawa kesa sa'yo?" some pax will instinctively say "Yes!" without thinking. Slow them down. "Bakit ka naniniwala?" If they cannot answer with a specific area where God did more than they could — they don't actually believe it yet; they're performing the right answer.
2 Questions in Order
  1. Bakit kaya negative ang perception ng mundo sa pagsuko? (Easier — diagnostic of the world's frame. Warm them up.)
  2. Naniniwala ka ba kay Moody? (Sharper — diagnostic of their own frame. Honest answer needed.)
Soft Prompts When Stuck
  • If only one person talks: "Salamat. Sino pa? Si [name]?"
  • If discussion gets theological/abstract: "Sa specific area ng buhay mo, bakit hirap mag-yield?"
  • If someone admits real struggle ("nahihirapan akong isuko ang trabaho ko"): Don't fix. "Salamat sa honesty. Ipagdarasal kita."
⚠️ The trap to avoid
Do NOT turn this into a Pharisaical "who has surrendered the most" contest. There is no winner. The table that admits the most genuine resistance is the most honest table — and the most ready for the ritual.

5
GAWIN · 3-Sangkap + Signed-Paper Ritual (15 min)
The lesson's load-bearing artifact + emotional climax
▼
⭐ The single most important thing today
When the facilitator hands out the 3-Sangkap Inventory, visibly fill your own out alongside the pax. Hold the pen. Read each prompt. Pause. Write. Especially Sangkap 2 — name a real area still being held in your own life. If you cannot do this with integrity in front of your table, you cannot ask them to do it. The pax will mimic your honesty or your performance.
The 3 Sangkap — What to Watch For Per Sangkap
1
Masaganang Habag ng Dios
Watch for: Pax who write "salvation" generically. Soft prompt: "Anong specific ginawa sa'yo ni Lord this year that you didn't deserve?" Pax who cannot name specific mercy is a pastoral flag — they may not perceive grace clearly.
2
Malayang Handog ng Tao
Watch for: The "everything-but-this" pax — those who can name surrender abstractly but cannot name a specific area still being held. Soft prompt: "Sa lahat ng bagay sa buhay mo, alin ang pinaka-mahirap isuko?" Acceptable answer: a specific name (person, project, dream, fear). Insufficient answer: "lahat" or "everything."
3
Malugod na Pagsamba at Paglilingkod
Watch for: Pax who compartmentalize Sunday from Monday-Saturday. Soft prompt: "Hindi sa Sunday lang. Sa Lunes mo umaga, paano ang work mo magiging worship?" Push for one specific Monday-Saturday action. Vague answers ("magiging maingat na ako") don't count.
⭐ The Signed-Paper Ritual — Reverence Protocol
⚠️ This is the emotional climax of L3. Read carefully.
1. After the facilitator tells the Sunday School story and hands out blank papers: Silently make sure every pax in your table has a paper + a working pen. No words.

2. During the writing: Look down. Do not watch them. Do not read their writing. Sign your own paper alongside.

3. During the 90-second silence after signing: Sit still. Do not fidget. Do not check your phone. Some pax will weep — do not approach them. Tissues should already be at the table. Let them be.

4. If someone openly sobs: Remain seated. Make brief eye contact. Small nod. Do not pray over them publicly. The Holy Spirit is doing the work. Pastor or facilitator handles direct ministry after the session if needed.

5. If someone refuses to sign: Do not pressure. Do not ask why. Note the name. Pastor follow-up — never confrontation.
Observation Checklist During GAWIN
  • Sinong pax ang nakapuno ng all 3 sangkap with specifics?
  • Sinong nag-skip ng Sangkap 2 o sumagot ng generic?
  • Sinong pumirma agad? Sinong nag-hesitate? Sinong hindi pumirma?
  • Sinong nag-show ng emotional response (tears, deep breath, body softening)?
  • Sinong nag-show ng resistance (rigid body, fast strokes, closed off)?

6
DALHIN · Hymn + EOLO Bridge + Send-Off (8 min)
Let Joy land
▼
During "I Surrender All"
Sing wholeheartedly. This is where Joy lands. The hymn's emotional arc — 5 verses of surrender → climax line "O the joy of full salvation!" — is the gospel logic of the entire lesson made musical. If you sing it like a funeral dirge, you betray the lesson. If you sing it like a celebration, you confirm it.
🌿 Why this hymn ends in joy, not solemnity
Romans 12:1 began with "alang-alang sa masaganang habag". The pax has just spent 15 minutes inventorying mercy received → handog offered → worship outflowing. The natural fruit of that arc is joy, not duty. Your face in the closing verses should reflect this. Tears of joy are fine. Smiles of relief are fine. Funeral face is wrong.
During the EOLO Bridge
Listen as the lead facilitator says "a surrendered life is the most evangelistic life." Your table will reflect — silent 60 seconds. Do not fill the silence. One of your pax may be realizing for the first time that their watching EOLO person has been observing them all year — and what they've been observing isn't the gospel they intended to share. That is sacred conviction. Let it land.
Your Role After the Closing Prayer
Walk over to the 1-2 pax at your table you flagged during the ritual — anyone who didn't sign, anyone who looked deeply moved, anyone who looked closed off. One sentence. No teaching.
🎤 Sample one-liners
For the deeply moved: "Kapatid, salamat sa pagiging open sa Holy Spirit ngayon. Ipinapanalangin kita this week."

For the closed-off / didn't sign: "Kapatid, walang pressure. Andyan ako ngayong linggo if you want to talk."

For the negotiation pax: nothing yet. Pastor follow-up.
Observation Checklist for DALHIN
  • Sinong pax sa table ko ang sumama sa pagkanta ng hymn nang may damdamin?
  • Sinong nag-underline ng linya sa lyrics?
  • Sinong umalis na may signed paper sa kamay or Bible? Sinong walang dala?

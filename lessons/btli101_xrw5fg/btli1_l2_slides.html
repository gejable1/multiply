📖 TUKLAS · Define Meditation
Meditation = Rumination
Kagaya ng baka — kumain, ngumuya, lulunukin, isusuka ulit, ngunguya muli — paulit-ulit hanggang ma-absorb.

Hebrew meaning ng meditation: "mamutawi lagi sa labi" — walang ibang bukambibig kundi ang Salita.
Contrast: Hindu meditation empties the mind. Biblical meditation fills it with God's thoughts.

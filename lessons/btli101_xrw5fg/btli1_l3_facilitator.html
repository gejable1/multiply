🌿 Character anchor for L3 — Joy (revised May 15, 2026)
Surrender is responsive love grounded in mercy received, climaxing in "O the joy of full salvation!" Romans 12:1 begins "alang-alang sa masaganang habag" — the lesson never frames surrender as self-control or gritted-teeth restraint. Carry the tone of Joy throughout, even in the gravity moments. Mercy received → mercy returned → joy of belonging.

The Opening Move — S.O.A.P.S Callback
⚡ Pukaw Activity 1 · ~3 min
30-Day S.O.A.P.S Callback
Setup: Stand up. No slides yet. Look at faces.

The ask: "Kumusta ang 30-day S.O.A.P.S challenge natin? Sino dito ang nag-pick ng John? Ilang chapters na kayo?"

What you're doing: Celebrating the rumination becoming rhythm. L2's character anchor was Faithfulness — daily return to the Word. If many are >10 days in, name it publicly. Two or three brief shares — voluntary. The rumination is becoming rhythm. Mark it.
🎯 Pastoral Note — The L1+L2 → L3 Arc
L1 was talking to God. L2 was listening to God. L3 is the question both lessons lead to: "what now?" The callback isn't decoration — it's the bridge. After hearing God for a month, the only honest response is the one Romans 12:1 names — surrender as responsive love to mercy received.
The Pivot — Mokusatsu
⚡ Pukaw Activity 2 · ~5 min
The World's Most Tragic Mistranslation
Tell the story: Hiroshima and Nagasaki. The Allied forces (Truman) demanded Japan's unconditional surrender. Reporters cornered Prime Minister Kantaro Suzuki — one word back: mokusatsu. Could mean "no comment," "disrespectful silence," or "I disregard." The translators picked the worst one. Allied forces escalated to atomic bombing. 200,000+ dead. "The world's most tragic case of mistranslation."

Then pause ~5 seconds. The weight is real. Do not joke through it.

Pivot: "Isang salita lang. Hindi malinaw ang pagsuko. Ang resulta — hindi kayang isukat. Paano kaya sa relasyon natin sa Dios? Ano nga ba ang unconditional surrender sa Christian life mo?"
⚠️ Gravity Handling
Do not show graphic images of the bombings. The text version of the story is enough. The story is a lesson about ambiguity in surrender, not about war. Use it; don't dwell in it.

~15 minutes · Guide discovery, do not lecture

The Big Idea (Slide)
🎯 Pangunahing Katotohanan
"Kung gusto nating pag-usapan ang Kaniyang pagsukob, dapat handa rin tayong talakayin ang ating pagsuko."
Surrender = Consecration

The English "surrender" carries baggage — feels like defeat. The better word: CONSECRATION. "Itinatalaga mo ang iyong buong buhay para sa paglilingkod at pagsamba sa Dios. Sa Kaniya ka lang — ABSOLUTELY, COMPLETELY, EXCLUSIVELY."

Why is consecration the better word? Because surrender suggests force; consecration suggests setting apart. The Lord is not occupying conquered territory. He is welcoming home what was always His.

1 Corinthians 6:19-20
"Hindi ninyo pag-aari ang inyong katawan; sapagkat binili na kayo sa isang halaga. Kaya't gamitin ninyo ang inyong katawan upang maparangalan ang Diyos."
Roma 14:7-8
"Walang sinuman sa atin ang nabubuhay o namamatay para sa sarili lamang... Kaya nga, sa mabuhay o sa mamatay, tayo'y sa Panginoon."

Pastoral framing: "Karapat-dapat lang na sumuko tayo sa Kaniya na nagligtas, nagpalaya, at nagmamahal sa atin. Sumuko tayo dahil ni minsan ay 'di Niya tayo sinukuan."

Pagsuko · Pagsunod · Pagsuway
Lucas 6:46-47
"Bakit ninyo ako tinatawag na 'Panginoon, Panginoon,' gayong hindi naman ninyo tinutupad ang sinasabi ko?... ang taong lumalapit sa akin, nakikinig sa aking mga salita, at nagsasagawa ng mga ito."

The sharp question: "Hindi mo puwedeng sabihin na ikaw ay sumusuko ng hindi ka sumusunod. It's either sumusunod o sumusuway. Tumatalima o tumatalikod. Alin ka sa dalawa?"

🎯 Pastoral Note — Why Joy is the Character Anchor
Surrender without joy becomes legalism. Surrender with joy is what Romans 12:1 actually teaches — "alang-alang sa masaganang habag" means BECAUSE of mercy received, not IN ORDER TO RECEIVE mercy. The disciple surrenders joyfully because they're already loved. Make this distinction explicit if a pax frames surrender as duty.

Movement 3 · TALAKAY
Discuss — World's View + Moody Challenge
~12 minutes · Table discussion, brief whole-room share
▼
Two Questions for Tables
📋 Tables of 4-6 · 8 minutes total
  1. Bakit kaya negative ang perception ng mundo sa pagsuko at pagpapasakop? Sa palagay niyo, bakit?
  2. Naniniwala ba kayo sa challenge ni D.L. Moody: "Isuko mo sa Dios ang iyong buhay; mas marami Siyang magagawa riyan kesa sa'yo."? Bakit o bakit hindi?

Pastoral framing of Q1: The world equates surrender with weakness because the world's value system is "langong-lango sa paghamig ng pansariling karapatan at kalayaan" — drunk on personal rights and freedom. In that logic, surrender = loss of agency. The gospel turns that on its head: the surrendered life is the only truly free life. Quote Augustine if useful: "You have made us for yourself, O Lord, and our hearts are restless until they rest in You."

Pastoral framing of Q2: The Moody quote is bait for the kind of disciple who still thinks of surrender as limitation. Moody is saying: the alternative to surrender — your own grip on your life — is the limitation. Yielding to God is opening to a wider life, not a narrower one.

Brief Whole-Room Share (3-4 min)

1-2 voluntary shares from a couple of tables. Don't force participation. The point is making the world's view visible so the disciple can recognize when they're carrying it unawares.

Setup for GAWIN — Hindi 50-50

Bridge to the GAWIN movement: "Sa Old Testament, kapag nag-alay ka, inaalis na ang lahat ng karapatan at claim mo. Hindi puwedeng 50-50. Hindi puwedeng laban ngayon, bawi maya-maya. Buong-buo — relationships, finances, pag-aaral, trabaho, lifestyle, pangarap, hobbies. Lahat."

Then transition: "Para hindi abstract, hatiin natin ang Romans 12:1 sa tatlong sangkap. Each one will inventory their own life through these three lenses."

Movement 4 · GAWIN

~15 minutes · The load-bearing artifact + emotional climax
▼
⭐ The load-bearing artifact of L3
The 3-Sangkap Inventory is what L3 builds toward and the signed-paper ritual is what L3 climaxes in. Give these the time they deserve. If you're running short, cut TALAKAY discussion shorter — never cut GAWIN.
The 3 Sangkap of Tunay na Pagsuko (Rom 12:1)

Hand out the 3-Sangkap Inventory sheet. Walk through one at a time. Give 2 minutes per sangkap for silent writing.

1
Masaganang Habag ng Dios
"alang-alang sa masaganang habag ng Diyos sa atin..."
The foundation — mercy already received. Apostle Paul addresses believers, not unbelievers (Rom 5:10 + 8:7 — the unbeliever cannot consecrate because their mind is set on the flesh). Surrender is the response of someone who has already received mercy.

Pax prompt: "Anong specific mercy ng Dios ang naranasan mo ngayong taon? Hindi generic. Pangalanan."

If a pax struggles: ask "sino sa pamilya mo ay buhay pa ngayon na hindi mo inakalang aabot ng ganitong taon?" Start where they are.
2
Malayang Handog ng Tao
"ialay ninyo ang inyong sarili bilang isang handog na buháy..."
The response — free-will offering. Not coerced (Paul uses "nakikiusap" — appealing, not commanding). Not 50-50. The OT sacrifices were dead; this is the New Testament version where the sacrifice lives. 2 Cor 5:14 — "ang pag-ibig ni Cristo ay pumipilit sa amin" — Christ's love is the pressure, not external rule.

Pax prompt: "Anong specific bahagi ng buhay mo ngayon ang inihahandog mo? Pangalanan ang isa. Hindi 'lahat' — pumili ng isa kung saan ka may hawak pa."

Watch for "everything-but-this" pax — those who name surrender abstractly but won't name a specific area. Gentle prompt: "Sa lahat ng bagay sa buhay mo, alin ang pinaka-mahirap isuko?"
3
Malugod na Pagsamba at Paglilingkod
"banal at kalugud-lugod sa Diyos. Ito ang karapat-dapat na pagsamba ninyo..."
The outflow — Monday-Saturday becomes worship. Worship is not just slow songs and Sunday service. Worship is the whole life given. "Kayo nga, kung kayo'y kumakain o umiinom, o anuman ang ginagawa ninyo, gawin ninyo ang lahat sa ikararangal ng Diyos" (1 Cor 10:31).

Pax prompt: "Paano magiging worship ang Monday-Saturday mo ngayong linggo? Hindi sa Sunday lang. Isa lang. Specific."

Connection to EOLO: Worship made visible to the watching world is the most evangelistic life. This is the bridge to DALHIN.
The Signed-Paper Ritual
⚡ Emotional Climax · 5 min
Hand out blank papers. Tell the Sunday School story.
The story (from TUGON p.27): "Minsan may isang batang babae na nagtanong sa kaniyang Sunday School teacher kung ano ba ang ibig sabihin ng 'pagsuko.' Kumuha ang teacher ng isang blangkong papel, iniabot sa bata at sinabing: 'Kapag pinirmahan at ibinigay mo ang papel ng iyong buhay sa Dios, at hinayaan mo Siyang magsulat ng gusto Niyang isulat dito — iyan ang pagsuko.'"

Instruction: "Each of you has a blank paper. Optionally, write a short prayer of surrender. Then sign your name at the bottom. That signature is your commitment. Fold the paper. Put it in your Bible at Romans 12:1 when you get home. Look at it weekly."

Play quiet instrumental music. Hold 90 seconds of silence after most have signed. Do not rush. The silence is the work.
⚠️ Pastoral Reverence
Some pax will weep during this ritual. Do not draw attention to them. Have tissues at every table beforehand. Do not pray over them publicly unless invited. The Holy Spirit is the one doing the work in this moment — your job is to not get in the way.

🪞 Debrief — 15-20 min within 48 hours of L3
Pastor + facilitator + interns gather. L3 is the most emotionally weighty lesson so far. Be honest. Look at the room with care.
For the Facilitator
1Ilang pax actually nag-share tungkol sa their 30-day S.O.A.P.S? May Day-14+ pax ba na ipinagdiwang publicly?
If <20% are past Day 7, L2's challenge didn't stick. Plan a more structured LCG follow-up before L4.
2Anong Sangkap (1/2/3) ang pinaka-nahirapan punuin ng pax? Ano sa palagay mo ang kahulugan?
Sangkap 1 hard = pax doesn't perceive mercy clearly (need pastoral encouragement). Sangkap 2 hard = pax has specific stronghold (gentle private follow-up). Sangkap 3 hard = pax compartmentalizes Sunday/M-Sat (need worship-as-life teaching).
3Sa signed-paper ritual — sino ang nagpakita ng emotional response? Sino ang nag-resist?
Both are data. Tears can mean breakthrough. Resistance can mean the Spirit is doing harder work and the pax needs time. Note names; pray for both.
4Anong specific surrender did you witness today that you did not expect?
Holy Spirit moments. Write them down. They become testimonies for L4 opener.
5Did anyone seem to be performing surrender rather than actually surrendering?
Sensitive question. Pax who can talk surrender fluently but cannot name a specific area in Sangkap 2 is a flag. Pastor follow-up — never confront in group.
For the Interns
6Sa table mo, sino ang pinaka-honest sa Sangkap 2 (the area still held)? Ano nilang sinabi?
Honesty in Sangkap 2 is the hardest. Specificity = trust. Name what you heard — but never share publicly outside the debrief.
7Sino ang nag-skip ng signing? Sino ang sign na malabo, parang formality lang?
Both are data. The skip = the Spirit said "not yet." The formality-sign = pax may not have understood. Don't judge either; just notice for next-month LCG conversations.
8Kung mag-fa-facilitate ka ng L3 sa LCG niyo next month, anong gagawin mong iba para mas maging weighty ang signed-paper ritual?
This question separates intern from leader. Specific. Concrete. Not "mas magiging emotional."
For Everybody
9What did we witness today that should be a testimony in next Sunday's service (with the pax's permission)?
Surrender moments are gold for celebration culture. But only with consent. Never volunteer someone else's story.
10What's the one specific commitment we make this week to follow up on what we saw?
One. Specific. Doable in 7 days. Could be: text a pax who looked moved, ask about their week. Could be: plan worship songs that echo "I Surrender All" for next Sunday. Could be: print extra blank papers for anyone who lost theirs.
🏠 LCG Weekly Mode
BTLI runs bi-monthly. LCG meets weekly. This pane is for the in-between weeks (Week 2 and Week 4 after L3 is taught). The goal: reinforce the surrender posture without re-teaching the lesson. L3 is the most emotionally weighty of the first three lessons — the weekly LCGs are where surrender is practiced in small relational moments.
5-Segment Weekly Agenda
1
KAMUSTAHAN + Memory Verse
~5 min
Quick check-ins. Then together recite Romans 12:1 from memory. If many struggle to recite, recite together as a group. The verse anchors every weekly LCG meeting for the duration of L3's cycle.
2
PAGDIRIWANG · Celebration
~7 min
The signed paper show-and-acknowledge. Each member opens their Bible to Rom 12:1 and shows whether the signed paper is still there. Don't read what's on it. Don't pressure. The point is visible faithfulness — and visible care from those who lost their paper. Have extra blank papers ready for anyone who lost theirs and wants to re-sign. No shaming.
3
PAGTUTULONG · Peer Help on Sangkap 2
~12 min
One member shares their Sangkap 2 — the specific area they named as still being held last month. Has any movement happened? The group responds: "Anong specific way puwede ka naming matulungan ngayong linggo para magawa mo iyan?" Facilitator only steps in if the group is stuck — and redirects: "Ano sa palagay niyo?" Trade weeks: different member each Wednesday LCG.
4
EOLO PULSE — The Watching Eyes
~10 min
One update per member on one EOLO name. Pegging it to L3: "Anong surrendered action ang nakita ng EOLO person mo from you this past week? Or anong opportunity ang dumating na kailangan mo isuko pa?" The point is making the Monday-Saturday witness visible. Pastoral follow-up data.
5
PANALANGIN PARA SA ISA'T ISA
~5 min
Pairs or trios. Each prays for the other's Sangkap 2 area from segment 3 and their EOLO person from segment 4. Specific prayer. Hindi generic. "Lord, tulungan Mo si [name] na maisuko ang [specific area] sa Biyernes pagdating niya sa [specific place]."
Week 2 vs Week 4 — Variation
📅 WEEK 2 — Just after L3
Segment 2 emphasis: Celebrate signing the paper. "Sino dito may signed paper sa Bible niya right now?" Adrenalin pa ng commitment.

Segment 3 emphasis: Initial surrender — what did the pax already DO this week from their Sangkap 2? Small wins.

Segment 4 emphasis: First-witness moments. "Did your EOLO person notice anything different about you?"
📅 WEEK 4 — Just before L4
Segment 2 emphasis: Celebrate persisting. Adrenalin gone. Joy mode — is the surrender still feeling like joy or has it slipped into duty? Honest names.

Segment 3 emphasis: Where has surrender been hardest? Specific moments of taking back what was given. No shame — just name.

Segment 4 emphasis: Deepening witness — did any EOLO conversation actually happen this week?
24-Hour Follow-up Message Template
Hi [Name], salamat sa LCG kagabi. Ipinagdarasal kita ngayong linggo para sa [Sangkap 2 area] mo — at para kay [EOLO name]. Bago mag-Sunday, padalhan mo ako ng isang short text — anong nangyari nung tinanong ka ni Lord tungkol sa [Sangkap 2 area]? Joy ka pa ba o nahirapan ka? Honesto lang. 🙏
Six Sources of Influence — How Each Segment Engages
  • Segment 1 (Memory verse): Personal Ability (recall) + Social Motivation (recited together) + Personal Motivation (Rom 12:1 grounds surrender in mercy received — re-anchors the WHY weekly).
  • Segment 2 (Signed-paper show): Structural Ability (the physical paper in the Bible makes commitment visible) + Social Motivation (peer witness).
  • Segment 3 (Sangkap 2 peer help): Social Ability (peer help) + Structural Ability (concrete weekly help offers).
  • Segment 4 (EOLO Pulse): Personal Motivation (love for the specific person) + Social Motivation (accountability) + Social Ability (the EOLO person becomes part of the disciple-maker's circle of influence).
  • Segment 5 (Prayer): Personal Motivation + Social Motivation + spiritual reliance.

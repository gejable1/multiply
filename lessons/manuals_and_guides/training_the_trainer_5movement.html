Pre-Session
Bago Magsimula · The Meta-Pedagogy Setup
15–20 minutes before the trainees arrive
▼
Read this first — what makes this lesson different

This is meta-pedagogy. You are teaching the 5-Movement Method by doing the 5-Movement Method. Your trainees will experience PUKAW, TUKLAS, TALAKAY, GAWIN, and DALHIN — and only after they've felt each one will you name what just happened.

This means two things:

  • Do not explain the method up front. Resist the urge to begin with "today we'll learn the five movements." That kills the experience. The structure of the session IS the lesson.
  • Name what's happening only at the end of each movement. "What you just experienced was PUKAW. Did you notice how the question created a felt need before any teaching?" After the experience, the label. Not before.
What You Need
Your Bible (Tagalog and/or English)
The MULTIPLY Facilitator's Manual (this session is its companion)
A copy of Usbong Lesson 1 facilitator file open on a phone or tablet — for the practicum
A journal or notebook + pen for each trainee
Comfortable, semi-private spot for 2 people (your home, a quiet café)
Comfortable space for 3–8 trainees (living room, classroom, café back room)
Spiritual Preparation

Pray for each trainee by name. Ask the Holy Spirit to give them — and you — the gift of experiencing this method, not just understanding it. The difference is everything. "Lord, let this not be a lecture. Let this be a moment they will remember when they later sit across from their own first learner. Form facilitators tonight, not students."

🎯 Who is this lesson for?
Future LC leaders, Usbong apprentices ready to facilitate, BTLI candidates, anyone who has completed at least one Usbong lesson as a learner and is ready to learn how to lead one. Don't try to train someone who hasn't yet experienced an Usbong session themselves. The method must be felt before it is taught.

Movement 1 · PUKAW
Engage — The Felt Need to Facilitate
10 minutes · 1-on-115 minutes · Small Group
▼
The Opening Question
👥 1-on-1 Mode
⚡ Pukaw Conversation
"Bakit ka nandito?"
Step 1 (2 min): Begin warmly. Ask: "Bakit ka nandito? Ano ang nag-akit sa iyo na pumunta dito at matuto kung paano mag-facilitate?" Wait. Listen.

Step 2 (3 min): Then ask a deeper question: "May naisip ka na bang specific na tao na gusto mong i-disciple? Kung meron — sino siya? Kung wala pa — sino kaya ang nais ng Diyos na ipakilala mo sa Kanya?" Let them name someone — even imperfectly. The naming matters.

Step 3 (3 min): The honest pivot: "Kapag nag-isip ka tungkol sa pagti-teach sa kanya — ano ang takot mo? Ano ang nararamdaman mo na hindi mo kaya?" Hear them out. Don't reassure too quickly.

Step 4 (2 min): Then say: "Maganda ang sagot mo. At alam mo kung bakit ako naniniwala na kaya mo? Hindi dahil magaling kang mag-teach. Kundi dahil ang ginagamit ng Diyos ay hindi 'yung pinaka-magaling — kundi 'yung pinaka-available. Ngayon, tingnan natin ang isang kuwento sa Bible kung paano ito gumagana."
👨‍👩‍👧 Small Group Mode
⚡ Pukaw Activity
"Ang Pinakamagaling na Guro Ko"
Step 1 (4 min): Give each trainee 3 minutes of silence to think and write in their journal: "Sino ang pinakamagaling na guro mo sa buong buhay mo — academic man, spiritual, o kahit sino? Anong dalawang katangian ang ginawang magaling siyang guro para sa iyo?"

Step 2 (6 min): Quick round — each person shares the name (or describes the person) and ONE of the two qualities they wrote. Keep it tight — 45 seconds each, max.

Step 3 (3 min): As facilitator, write the qualities visibly (a piece of paper, a whiteboard, or just verbally summarize). Then ask: "Pansinin niyo — ilan sa mga sinabi ninyo ay tungkol sa kung gaano kaalam ang teacher? At ilan ay tungkol sa kung paano kayo kanyang inalagaan, o pinakinggan, o sinamahan?"

Step 4 (2 min): Land it: "Karamihan sa pinakamagaling nating mga guro — hindi tayo binigyan ng impormasyon. Tayo ay sinamahan. Tonight, hindi kayo mag-aaral kung paano magbigay ng impormasyon. Mag-aaral kayo kung paano sumama. Tingnan natin sa Bible kung paano ito gumagana."
Bridge to TUKLAS
🎤 Your own words — taglish is natural
"Bago tayo pumunta sa Scripture, may isasabi muna ako sa inyo. Ang pinagdaanan natin sa nakaraang 10 minuto — iyan ay tinatawag na PUKAW. Ang ginawa ko ay hindi nagbibigay ng impormasyon. Ang ginawa ko ay nag-akit ng pakiramdam ng pangangailangan — paano ba talaga ang mag-disciple? Ito ang unang galaw ng method na pag-aaralan natin ngayon. Tara, lumipat tayo sa pangalawang galaw."
🎯 Pastoral Note — Naming what just happened
This is the meta-pedagogy moment. Right at the bridge, you label what they just experienced. "That was PUKAW." Don't lecture about it — just name it. The trainees will feel a small "ohhh!" of recognition. That recognition is the first deposit in their facilitator's bank. Throughout the session, you'll be making deposit after deposit — by Movement 5, they will have felt the whole method in their bodies.

Movement 2 · TUKLAS
Discover — Jesus as Facilitator
15 minutes25 minutes
▼
Scripture — Read Together
Luke 24:13–32 · The Road to Emmaus · Primary Text
"At sa parehong araw na iyon, dalawa sa kanila ay papunta sa isang nayon na pinagngangalanang Emaus... Habang sila ay nag-uusap at nagtatalo, si Jesus mismo ay lumapit at sumamahan sila... 'Anong ipinag-uusap ninyo?' Tumigil sila, may lungkot sa kanilang mukha... Pagkatapos, simula sa Moises at sa lahat ng mga Propeta, ipinaliwanag Niya sa kanila ang mga sinabi tungkol sa Kanyang sarili sa lahat ng Kasulatan."
"And it happened that as they conversed and discussed, Jesus himself drew near and went with them... 'What is this conversation that you are holding with each other?' They stood still, looking sad... Then beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, he interpreted to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning himself."
📖 Reading Method · 1-on-1
Read verses 13-17 yourself, slowly. Then have your trainee read 18-27 aloud. Then both of you read 28-32 silently in your own Bibles. Three readings, three voices, three modes. The text is too long for one reading.
📖 Reading Method · Small Group
Assign three readers: one for verses 13-17 (the setup), one for 18-27 (Jesus joins them), one for 28-32 (the recognition). Each reader stands as they read. The standing matters — it differentiates voices and dignifies the text.
Discovery Questions
🗣 Discovery Discovery
Ask each question; do not answer it yourself. Wait. Re-read the verse if they freeze. The trainees must discover what Jesus does as facilitator — because they're about to be told "this is the method."
  • OBSERVATION: "In verses 13-17 — what does Jesus do FIRST when He meets the two disciples? Does He immediately teach them? What does He do?" Guide toward: He walks beside them. He asks a question. He listens. He doesn't lead with content.
  • INTERPRETATION: "Look at the question Jesus asks in verse 17 — 'What is this conversation that you are holding with each other?' Why this question? Why not jump straight to telling them who He is?" Guide toward: He surfaces their felt need. He lets them name their grief and confusion before He brings the truth. That IS the method.
  • APPLICATION: "When you sit across from someone you're discipling — your future EOLO person — what would it look like for you to do what Jesus does here? To walk beside instead of teach AT?" Let them answer. Their answer IS the lesson.
Bridge to TALAKAY
🎤 Naming the second movement
"Ang ginawa natin ngayon — ang pagtatanong, hindi pag-tuturo, hindi pag-bigay ng sagot — iyan ay tinatawag na TUKLAS. Ito ang pangalawang galaw ng method. Sa Tuklas, hinahayaan natin ang Scripture na magsalita muna bago tayo. Hinahayaan natin ang tao na makita mismo. Si Jesus mismo ang nag-tatag ng pattern na ito. Tara — pumunta tayo sa pangatlong galaw."
🎯 Pastoral Note — Resist explaining
You will be tempted to explain the Emmaus passage in detail. You will want to say "here's what Jesus is doing theologically, here's what we learn about His ministry style, here's how it applies to us." Resist. The trainees discovered it — that's the point. By preserving the discovery, you are showing them what TUKLAS means. If you over-explain, you contradict the very method you're teaching.

Movement 3 · TALAKAY
Deepen — The Cost of Being a Guide
15 minutes25 minutes
▼
Part A · The Truth (3 min)
💡 Say This Plainly
"Para maging guide, kailangan munang sumuko ng pagiging hero."
"To be a guide, you must first surrender being a hero."

The hero says: "I have the answers, follow me." The guide says: "I am one step ahead of you, walk with me." The hero wants admiration. The guide wants the learner to reach the destination — and is comfortable being forgotten when they do.

Don't add more. Let it sit.
Part B · Your Story (5 min · mutual)(10 min · facilitator + 1-2 trainees)
👥 1-on-1 Mode
📖 Mutual Story Exchange
Step 1 (3 min): Share your own honest moment — a time you tried to be a hero in someone's faith journey and it didn't work, or a time you were a quiet guide and it bore fruit. Brief. Specific. Vulnerable, not polished.

Step 2 (2 min): Ask: "May naisip ka bang sandali sa iyong buhay nung may isang taong sinubukang i-disciple ka — at gumana ito o hindi? Anong ginawa nilang tama o mali?" Listen.
👨‍👩‍👧 Small Group Mode
📖 Story + Story
Step 1 (4 min): You tell a brief, vulnerable story. Use Before / Moment / After. "Bago ko natutunan ito, ako'y nag-aral kung paano mag-deliver ng perfect sermon... Nung pinatry kong gawin ito kay [name]... Pagkatapos, natutunan ko na..." Keep it under 4 minutes. Modeling vulnerability is the lesson here.

Step 2 (6 min): Open invitation: "May 1 o 2 ba sa inyo na may katulad na karanasan na nais ibahagi? Hindi obligated lahat — voluntary lang." Take 1-2 stories. Don't go around the room.
Part C · Personal Reflection (7 min · silence)(11 min · silence + journal)
💡 Sariling Pagmumuni-muni
Say: "May tatlong tanong na nasa isip ko para sa iyo. Hindi mo kailangang i-share sa akin. Sagutin mo sa journal mo. Pitong minuto."

Then say nothing more. Sit with them. Use the time to pray silently for each name your trainee has mentioned.

The three questions to give:
  1. "Kanino sa buhay ko ako nais maging hero? Bakit?"
  2. "Anong takot ang humahadlang sa akin para maging guide na lang?"
  3. "Kung makikita ako ng tagapag-disciple ko sa loob ng 5 taon, anong klaseng tao ang nais kong makita nila?"
Say: "May tatlong tanong na nasa isip ko para sa inyo. Hindi obligated mag-share — sagutin niyo sa journal niyo. Labing-isang minuto. Walang chat, walang phone."

Then say nothing more. Stand or sit calmly. Use the silence to pray for each trainee by name.

The three questions to give:
  1. "Kanino sa buhay ko ako nais maging hero? Bakit?"
  2. "Anong takot ang humahadlang sa akin para maging guide na lang?"
  3. "Kung makikita ako ng tagapag-disciple ko sa loob ng 5 taon, anong klaseng tao ang nais kong makita nila?"
🎯 Pastoral Note — Honor the silence
Some trainees will look up, glance at you, wait for you to break the silence. Smile gently and look down at your own Bible. Hold the space.Someone in the group will try to break the silence with a joke or sigh. Gently raise your hand a few inches off the table — signal "wait." Hold the space. What you are doing right now is teaching them how to hold silence as facilitators. They are watching you hold space, even though their eyes are mostly on their journal. The way you hold this silence will become the way they hold silence with their own future learners.
Bridge to GAWIN
🎤 Naming the third movement
"Ang ginawa natin — ang truth, ang istorya, ang katahimikan — iyan ay tinatawag na TALAKAY. Ito ang pinaka-mahalagang galaw para sa pagbabago ng puso. Doon nangyayari ang Holy Spirit na trabaho. Ngayon naman, kailangan natin gawin ang isang bagay — kasi ang lahat ng aral na walang ginagawa ay nakalimutan lamang. Tara — practicum tayo."

Movement 4 · GAWIN
Practice — Facilitate a Movement Right Now
25 minutes35 minutes
▼
The Practicum — facilitate ONE movement of Usbong Lesson 1
🙌 The Heart of This Training
"Subukin mo na — kahit isang movement lang"
Open the Usbong Lesson 1 facilitator file on your phone or tablet. The trainee/trainees will actually facilitate one movement from it — usually PUKAW (the shortest, lowest-stakes), with you playing the learner.

This is the moment training becomes formation. Reading about the method is one thing. Doing it once is another thing entirely.
🎯 1-on-1 Practicum
  1. Setup (3 min): Open Usbong Lesson 1 facilitator file. Have your trainee read the PUKAW section silently for 2 minutes. They are about to facilitate it for you.
  2. Round 1 — They facilitate PUKAW for you (8 min): You play the role of "someone who comes to church but doesn't really know God personally." Let them lead the celebrity-vs-friend conversation. Stay in character. Don't help them.
  3. Debrief Round 1 (5 min): Three questions. "Anong nararamdaman mong gumana?" Let them name it. "Anong parte ang nahirapan ka?" Don't critique; just receive. "Anong gagawin mong iba next time?" Their answer is the lesson.
  4. You affirm ONE specific thing they did well (1 min): Concrete. "Pansinin ko, hindi mo sinabi 'tama' o 'mali' sa sagot ko. Ang tinanong mo, 'pa-explain mo nga'. Iyan ang ginagawa ng guide, hindi ng hero."
  5. Optional Round 2 (8 min) — if time allows: They facilitate TUKLAS (just the discovery questions). Same debrief cycle.
🎯 Small Group Practicum
  1. Setup (3 min): Each trainee has the Usbong Lesson 1 facilitator file on their phone. Pair them off (if odd number, you join the leftover). Each pair will take turns facilitating PUKAW for each other.
  2. Round 1 (10 min): Person A facilitates PUKAW for Person B. Person B plays a learner. After 8 minutes, swap.
  3. Round 2 (10 min): Person B facilitates PUKAW for Person A. Person A plays a learner.
  4. Pair debrief (5 min): Each pair discusses: "Anong gumana? Anong nahirapan ka? Anong gagawing iba next time?"
  5. Group debrief (7 min): Bring everyone back. "Sino sa inyo nakatuklas ng isang bagay na hindi pa nila alam tungkol sa pagiging facilitator?" 2-3 voluntary shares. Affirm specifics. Don't critique anyone publicly.
Bridge to DALHIN
🎤 Naming the fourth movement
"Ang ginawa natin — ang practice, ang debrief, ang specific affirmation — iyan ay tinatawag na GAWIN. Ang lahat ng natutunan natin sa unang tatlong galaw ay nawawala kung hindi natin ginagawa kaagad. Ngayon — bago tayo umuwi — kailangan mong dalhin ang isang bagay. Tara, last movement."
🎯 Pastoral Note — On critique
Your trainees will make mistakes during the practicum. They will fumble questions, give lecturish answers, miss obvious cues. Do not correct them in the moment. Affirm what worked. Save corrections for one-on-one feedback after the session, or save them for the NEXT training cycle. Public correction shames; private affirmation builds. Remember the principle: the way you treat them now is the way they will treat their own learners.

Movement 5 · DALHIN SA BAHAY
Send — Schedule Your First Real Session
10 minutes · Assignment + Verse + Pangako + Close
▼
The Assignment
🏠 Takdang-Aralin · Sa Loob ng Dalawang Linggo
Schedule and facilitate ONE real Usbong Lesson 1 session
Sa loob ng dalawang linggo mula ngayon — schedule and run ONE Usbong Lesson 1 session with a real person (or small group). Not practice. Not roleplay. An actual session.

"Sino ang isa? Kailan? Saan?" — Have them say the name, date, and location aloud right now. Specific commitments survive; vague commitments evaporate.

After the session, message your trainer one paragraph: "Sino, kailan, anong gumana, anong nahirapan ka."
Memory Verse
📝 Talata ng Memorya
Lucas 6:40
"Ang isang alagad ay hindi hihigit sa kanyang guro, ngunit ang bawat isa, kapag lubos nang nasanay, ay magiging katulad ng kanyang guro."
📝 Memory Verse Drill
  1. Read it together aloud — twice
  2. Cover the verse. Say it from memory together
  3. You and your trainee recite it to each other without looking
  4. Pairs in the group recite it to each other without looking
  5. Have them screenshot the verse card or write it in their journal
Pangako Ko · My Commitment
🎤 Lead this — no rushing
"Bago tayo mag-close, gusto kong makuha ang pangako mopangako ninyo. Sa journal mo: 'Sa susunod na dalawang linggo, ifa-facilitate ko ang Usbong Aralin 1 para kay _______, sa __________ (lugar), sa _______ (petsa). Magti-trust ako sa method, kahit hindi ako sigurado.'"
The Closing Ritual — Same Every Session
🕊 Closing · 1-on-1
  1. Stand or sit facing each other
  2. Say Lucas 6:40 together from memory — final time
  3. Place your hand on their shoulder. Pray specifically for them BY NAME, and for the specific person they committed to disciple
  4. Look them in the eye. Say: "Facilitator ka na, kapatid. Magdi-disciple ka rin."
  5. Confirm the date you'll check back in (suggest 2 weeks). Walk out together if possible.
🕊 Closing · Small Group
  1. Everyone stands in a small circle
  2. Say Lucas 6:40 together from memory — final time
  3. You pray specifically — name each trainee and the specific person they committed to disciple
  4. Everyone together, hands at heart or raised: "Disciples who make disciples."
  5. Remind: assignment is within 2 weeks. Confirm the next check-in date. Same closing ritual every future training.
🎯 The Closing Ritual Matters
Notice what you just did: this closing ritual is the same one used in every Usbong lesson. The trainees will recognize it. By using the same closing in their training, you've installed the muscle memory they'll need when they run their own first session. They'll close with "Disciples who make disciples" — and feel themselves connected to every other facilitator who has ever said it.
🌱 After the session — your follow-up
Within 24 hours: Send your trainee a brief message. "Salamat sa training kagabi. Praying for you and ___ (the name they committed to). Magpa-update ka sa akin pagkatapos."

2 weeks later: Check in. Ask how it went. Listen more than you teach. Their first real session is sacred. Within 24 hours: Send the group a brief message. "Salamat sa lahat sa training. Praying for each of you and your committed disciples."

2 weeks later: Schedule a follow-up gathering — small, casual, just to debrief their first real session. This is when most of the learning actually happens. Don't skip the follow-up.

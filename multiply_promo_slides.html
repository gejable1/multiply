Act One

The Member Tool

Ang gamit mo sa paglago

Your walk with God, in your pocket. Not a church app full of notices — a place to grow.

📖
Every day

Devotional

Araw-araw na Salita

Open it. Read the passage. Then write what God said to you — your reflection is saved, and it stays yours.

Basahin. Pagnilayan. Isulat. Hindi ka mag-iisa sa paglalakbay — ang bawat araw ay may Salita para sa iyo.
🎓
Learn

Lessons & Quizzes

Usbong · BTLI

Watch the video. Read the lesson. Take the quiz — 70% to pass, and three tries, because this is formation, not an exam.

Your leader can see that you finished. Nobody has to chase you for a piece of paper.

🙏
Bring it to God

Prayer

Panalangin ng araw · Kahilingan

Send a prayer request straight to your leader. And here is the part that matters: when someone prays for you, it is recorded.

Hindi lang ito nakalista sa isang papel. May taong nagdadasal para sa'yo — at alam mo iyon.
👣
Journey · Lakbay

See how far you've come

The pathway shows every step of growth — and marks each one you complete. You are not wandering. You are being led somewhere.

Not a leaderboard. Nobody is ranked. This is your road, and only yours.

🌱
Discover · Tuklas

How God made you

Spiritual gifts. Strengths. How you work, and how you handle conflict. Find out where you fit — so you serve from who you are, not from who's available.

🌍
EOLO

One person. Prayed for. Followed up. Loved.

Hindi mo kailangang abutin ang buong mundo. Isa lang. Kung ang bawat isa sa atin ay may isa — mararating natin ang lahat.
🔔

Finish a step and your discipler knows — not to check up on you, but to celebrate with you. Growth that nobody notices is a lonely thing.

Act Two

The Leader Tool

Hindi ito dashboard. Bantayan ito.

This is not a dashboard for managing people. It is a watchtower for keeping them.

👥
My Group

Your people, by name

Who they are. Where they are on the pathway. What they finished last week. Everything you need to shepherd them — in one place.

📊
Spiritual Vitality

A care lens. Not a scoreboard.

The Spiritual Vitality Index does not rank your members and it does not grade their walk with God.

It answers one question, and only one: who has gone quiet, and needs a visit?

Ang numero ay hindi hatol. Ito ay paanyaya na dalawin ang isang tao.
👣
Pathway

Encourage. Track. Celebrate.

See every step your disciples take, and mark it with them. You bear witness to their growth — you never certify it. Only God grows a person.

And when one of them finishes something, you will be the first to know — so you can be the first to rejoice.

🌱
Assessment Review

Place them where they'll flourish

Read your members' spiritual gifts and strengths. Stop filling slots with whoever says yes. Put people where God already built them to thrive.

✅
Attendance & Heads-ups

Notice who is missing

Log the meeting in seconds. And when someone slips away, you are told — not so you can scold them, but so you can go and find them.

Ang isang tupa na nawawala ay hindi isang numero. Siya ay hinahanap.
📖
Meeting Prep

Sunday's devotional, ready by Tuesday

Turn the daily devotional into a full one-hour LC study — facilitator guide and participant guide, English and Tagalog. Prepare in minutes, not hours.

📣
Reach them

Announcements & Links

Send the word out. See who has received it. Send each member their own private link — no group chat, no lost message.

📈
Reports

Know your flock

Attendance. Quizzes. Pathway progress. The truth about your group, whenever you ask for it.

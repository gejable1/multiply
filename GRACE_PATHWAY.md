# GRACE PATHWAY

**Rosehill Christian Church — Discipleship Framework**

**Purpose:** This file is the canonical, theological-and-structural definition of the GRACE Pathway, the discipleship framework that powers MULTIPLY. It integrates with the **3Ms**, the **Five Stages of Discipleship**, and the **5-Level Leadership Pipeline** (see `MULTIPLY_PIPELINE_DIAGRAM.md`).

This file is reference material — not operational state, not implementation. When building UI, lessons, or curricula that reference GRACE stages, this file is the source of truth.

**Last reviewed:** May 14, 2026 (initial extraction)

---

## 🎯 THE THREE INTERLOCKING FRAMEWORKS

MULTIPLY is built on three frameworks that map onto one another so that every member, in every stage, can name where they are and what their next step is.

| Framework | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **The 3Ms** | **Right Mission** (Great Commission), **Right Model** (5-Level Leadership Pipeline), **Right Method** (EOLO — Each One Love One — relational, life-on-life disciplemaking). All three must be present and aligned. |
| **Five Stages of Discipleship** | Dead → Infant → Children → Young Adult → Parent. Every church member can identify where they are and what their next step is. |
| **GRACE Pathway** | A 5-stage pathway: **G**ather → **R**eceive → **A**nchor → **C**ultivate → **E**xtend. Maps the full journey from the lost to the multiplying leader. |

## G — GATHER

**Theme:** *Prayer & Outreach — someone is being prayed for and invited.*
**Discipleship stage:** Dead moving toward Infant.
**Pipeline level:** Pre-Pipeline.
**Key verse:** Luke 15:4 — *"Suppose one of you has a hundred sheep and loses one of them. Doesn't he leave the ninety-nine in the open country and go after the lost sheep until he finds it?"*

### Core idea
Every member of the church is a missionary on a small mission field — usually 3 to 5 people in their natural relational orbit (family, neighbors, classmates, co-workers, friends). The Gather stage is about loving these people **before** any conversation about Christ — bringing them into prayer, presence, and friendship.

### The BLESS prayer & practice model
A simple, memorable rhythm every Christian can practice without specialized training:
- **B** — **Begin with prayer** (intercede daily for your "one")
- **L** — **Listen** (genuinely hear their story, struggles, longings)
- **E** — **Eat together** (build trust through hospitality and shared meals)
- **S** — **Serve** (look for tangible ways to bless them)
- **S** — **Share** (when the door opens, share the Gospel naturally)

### Connection to EOLO
GATHER is the operational stage of the **Each One Love One** campaign. Every member commits to praying for and loving one person toward Christ. The platform tracks these prayer relationships and supports them with AI-assisted reminders, conversation prompts, and reflection.

### Stage transition milestone
The person attends a church event — Sunday service, EGR retreat, LCG, kids' ministry, evangelistic concert, etc. — for the first time. **Celebrate this.** A first-timer attending is the visible fruit of GATHER.

## R — RECEIVE

**Theme:** *Salvation & Assurance — new birth.*
**Discipleship stage:** Infant.
**Pipeline level:** Pre-Pipeline.
**Key verse:** John 1:12 — *"Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God."*

### Core idea
RECEIVE is the moment (or season — sometimes salvation unfolds gradually in awareness) when a person trusts Christ. This stage is not just about the decision — it is about establishing **assurance** so the new believer is not blown about by feelings, doubts, or accusations.

### The salvation conversation framework
A simple Gospel presentation flow taught to every Anchor-stage believer so they can articulate it for someone else:
1. **God's design** — He made us for relationship with Him (Gen 1:27).
2. **Our problem** — Sin broke that relationship (Rom 3:23).
3. **God's solution** — Christ died for our sins and rose again (Rom 5:8, 1 Cor 15:3–4).
4. **Our response** — Repent and believe (Mark 1:15; John 1:12).

### The Salvation Assurance Diagnostic
The MULTIPLY platform includes a 5-area diagnostic (based on 1 John and Romans) to help a new believer gain confidence in their salvation:
1. **Belief in Christ's person and work** (1 John 5:1)
2. **Conviction of sin and ongoing repentance** (1 John 1:8–9)
3. **Love for the brethren** (1 John 3:14)
4. **Witness of the Spirit** (Rom 8:16)
5. **Obedience and fruit** (1 John 2:3; Gal 5:22–23)

The diagnostic is a **conversation starter, NOT a verdict** (see Invariant #16). The pastor reviews flagged results on his own initiative.

### Stage transition milestone
The person makes a credible profession of faith and is enrolled into the Anchor track. **Celebrate this publicly** — salvations are the heartbeat of a disciplemaking church.

## A — ANCHOR

**Theme:** *Foundation — basics of faith, connect to community.*
**Discipleship stage:** Children (Spiritual Children — feeding themselves on milk, learning the basics).
**Pipeline level:** Team Member (Lead Self).
**Key verse:** Colossians 2:6–7 — *"So then, just as you received Christ Jesus as Lord, continue to live your lives in him, rooted and built up in him, strengthened in the faith as you were taught, and overflowing with thankfulness."*

### Core idea
A new believer has been born again but is still spiritually fragile. ANCHOR is the season of being **rooted, built up, strengthened, and overflowing** — establishing the practices, relationships, and convictions that make their faith sustainable.

### The 6-Module Foundation Curriculum (Anchor track)
Designed to be delivered by an Anchor Mentor in either a 1-on-1 or LCG context:
1. **Assurance of Salvation** (revisits the Salvation Assurance Diagnostic)
2. **The Word of God** (daily bible reading, devotional writing, the church devotional guide)
3. **Prayer** (talking with God — adoration, confession, thanksgiving, supplication)
4. **The Church** (why community is non-negotiable; LCG culture; membership)
5. **Baptism** (obedient public identification with Christ)
6. **Witnessing** (sharing your testimony; entering the Gather stage as a sender, not just a receiver)

### Integration: EGR Retreat
The redesigned 2-day **EGR (Experiencing God Retreat)** is now an evangelism program intended for first-timers (Gather → Receive transition) rather than only existing believers. For an Anchor-stage believer, attending EGR with their own "one" becomes a Gather-stage practice.

### Daily disciplines (begin at Anchor, continue lifelong)
- **Daily bible reading and devotional writing** using the published church devotional guide
- **Weekly LCG attendance**
- **Sunday worship**
- **One ongoing prayer relationship** (their first "one" in EOLO)

### Anchor Mentor pairing system
Every new believer is paired with an Anchor Mentor — typically a Cultivate-stage or Extend-stage disciple. The pairing is tracked in MULTIPLY via `discipler_id`. The mentor's role is **relational, not instructional** — walking with the new believer through the 6 modules over roughly 12 weeks.

### Stage transition milestone
- The 6 modules are complete
- Baptism is observed (if not already)
- The believer is consistently attending LCG and practicing daily devotions
- They can articulate the Gospel and their testimony
- They are praying for and loving their first "one"

They are now ready to enter CULTIVATE as a Team Member in some area of ministry.

## C — CULTIVATE

**Theme:** *Discipleship — growing in character & competence.*
**Discipleship stage:** Young Adult (Spiritual Young Adult — God-centered, others-centered, learning to serve and lead).
**Pipeline level:** Leader → Coach (Mac Lake levels 2 and 3).
**Key verse:** Luke 6:40 — *"A student is not above the teacher, but everyone who is fully trained will be like their teacher."*

### Core idea
CULTIVATE is the long, slow, beautiful work of being **fully trained** — the work of a lifetime, but with measurable progressions. Two dimensions move together: **character** (Gal 5:22–23) and **competence** (gifting-appropriate skills for leadership).

### Sub-levels within Cultivate
| Sub-level | Pipeline Name | Role Examples |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| **C1 — Team Member** | Leads Self | Ministry team member (any ministry except Music, Teaching, Preaching, which require Leader-level) |
| **C2 — Leader** | Leads Others | LC Leader, Worship Leader, Devotional Preacher, Campus Minister, Sunday School Teacher |
| **C3 — Coach** | Leads Leaders | Sunday Preacher, Seminar Lecturer, Trainor, Council Member, BTLI Lecturer |

### Character: the Fruit of the Spirit (Gal 5:22–23)
Each fruit is taught not as an abstract virtue but as a measurable life practice in the disciple's relationships, work, and habits:
- **Love** — actions of self-giving toward the inconvenient
- **Joy** — gladness rooted in God's character, not circumstances
- **Peace** — shalom-bringing in conflict; non-anxious presence
- **Patience** — long-suffering with people who are slow to change (including yourself)
- **Kindness** — generous attention and warmth in ordinary interactions
- **Goodness** — moral integrity, especially when no one is watching
- **Faithfulness** — keeping commitments past the point of feeling
- **Gentleness** — power held under restraint
- **Self-control** — mastery of desires (food, words, money, sex, time, screen)

### Competence: aligned to Mac Lake's pipeline
At each sub-level, specific competencies are developed:
- **Team Member** — Leading Yourself: spiritual disciplines, time stewardship, self-awareness, accountability
- **Leader** — Leading Others: shepherding a small team, situational leadership, raising up leaders, problem-solving
- **Coach** — Leading Leaders: 1-on-1 development conversations, encouraging leaders, decision-making

### The Triad of Development (Mac Lake)
Every Cultivate-stage disciple grows through:
- **KNOWLEDGE** — what to know (lessons, books, sermons)
- **EXPERIENCE** — what to practice (real ministry assignments)
- **COACHING** — what to be corrected on (1-on-1 development conversations)

Knowledge or experience alone reinforces bad habits. Only the triad produces transformation.

### Six Sources of Influence (Grenny et al.) — applied to life transformation
Built into every Cultivate-stage development plan:
| | Motivation | Ability |
|---|------------|---------|
| **Personal** | Connect to values | Surpass your limits |
| **Social** | Harness peer pressure | Find strength in numbers |
| **Structural** | Design rewards & accountability | Change the environment |

A single source rarely produces change. Lasting transformation requires several sources operating together — a principle MULTIPLY's lesson design will reflect at every level.

### FATS — the candidate filter for leadership
For C2 and above, the best candidates are **FATS**:
- **F**aithful — keeps their word; consistent over time
- **A**vailable — has margin and is willing to be deployed
- **T**eachable — receives correction without defending
- **S**elfless — uses authority to serve, not to be served

### Required training pathways through Cultivate
- **C1 (Team Member):** BTLI 101 (Leading Yourself); EGR; Usbong; Usad
- **C2 (Leader):** BTLI 102, 103 (Leading Others); IDC; books: *Unlad*, *Fresh Wind Fresh Fire*
- **C3 (Coach):** BTLI 104 (Leading Leaders); IDC; APIIS; Clifton Strengths; books: *Mere Christianity*, *Still Sovereign*, *Happy Lies*
- **Future preachers:** CCF Glorious Hope program; APIIS Masters Program

### Stage transition milestones
Each sub-level has its own milestone, but the broad Cultivate → Extend transition occurs when a person is **regularly reproducing other disciples** — not just leading them, but watching them disciple others (the "spiritual parent" mark).

## E — EXTEND

**Theme:** *Multiplication — making disciples who make disciples.*
**Discipleship stage:** Parent (Spiritual Parent — reproducing).
**Pipeline level:** Director → Executive Leader (Mac Lake levels 4 and 5).
**Key verse:** 2 Timothy 2:2 — *"And the things you have heard me say in the presence of many witnesses entrust to reliable people who will also be qualified to teach others."*

### Core idea
EXTEND is the stage of **multiplication, not just addition**. An Extend-stage disciple is producing reproducing disciples. Their ministry is measured not by what they themselves do but by what their disciples' disciples are doing.

### Sub-levels within Extend
| Sub-level | Pipeline Name | Role Examples |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| **E1 — Director** | Leads a Department / Ministry | Ministry Director, Life Coach, Core Group Member |
| **E2 — Executive Leader** | Leads an Organization / Church | Pastor, Missionary, Church Planter |

### Six Sources of Influence — applied to leadership
At the Extend level, the disciple isn't just being influenced — they are **designing influence** for the disciples and leaders below them. They learn to deploy all six sources intentionally in the systems they oversee.

### Required training pathways through Extend
- **E1 (Director):** BTLI 105; IDC Coaching Training; counseling training; books: *Leadership Coaching* (Tony Stoltzfus), *The Disciplines, Skills and Heart of a Christian Coach* (Keith E. Webb)
- **E2 (Executive Leader):** BTLI 106; IDC; Church Planting Training; books: *Why Men Hate Going to Church*, *Christian Coaching: Helping Others Turn Potential into Reality (2nd ed.)*

### Legacy planning
Every Extend-stage disciple is asked: *"Who are the 2–3 people you are entrusting your ministry to?"* (2 Tim 2:2). This is non-negotiable. A leader without successors has not yet finished the work of EXTEND.

## 🎉 THE CELEBRATION CULTURE

GRACE is not just a pipeline — it is a **community journey**. Every stage transition is celebrated publicly. The church should celebrate:
- **First-time visitors** (GATHER stage reached)
- **Salvations** (RECEIVE stage)
- **Baptisms** (ANCHOR milestone)
- **LCG launches** (CULTIVATE milestones)
- **Leadership commissionings** (EXTEND stage)
- **Church plants and missionary sendings** (EXTEND stage completion)

Celebration is what makes the disciplemaking culture **visible**. A newcomer should notice within minutes of walking in that this church celebrates spiritual progress the way other communities celebrate athletic or academic wins.

## 📚 FOR LESSON & CURRICULUM DESIGNERS

When designing any new lesson, retreat, devotional, or training, ask:
1. **Which GRACE stage is this for?** (G, R, A, C, or E)
2. **Which sub-level within that stage?** (e.g., C1 vs C2 vs C3)
3. **Which dimension does it develop?** (Character, competence, or both)
4. **Which sources of influence does it engage?** (At least 2–3 for transformation)
5. **What is the next step it points to?** (Every lesson moves the disciple forward)
6. **How will completion be celebrated?**

## 🎯 TWO VIEWS OF THE PATHWAY: TRACKABLE STEPS vs REFERENCE RUNGS

**This is the single most confusing thing about the pathway, even for its own designers. Read this before editing rungs.**

The pipeline is shown in two different places that answer two different questions. They are NOT two views of the same list.

| | **Leadership Pipeline poster** (`leadership_pipeline.html`, in MD) | **My pathway** (MMT, and the marking view in MLT) |
|---|---|---|
| Question it answers | *What does the whole journey look like?* | *What are the next steps for THIS person?* |
| Shows | **Every** rung, all categories, all levels | Only the **trackable** rungs at the disciple's level |
| Role | The vision on the wall | The checklist in the hand |
| Reads the `trackable` flag? | **No -- it shows everything** | **Yes -- trackable rungs only** |

So when a rung appears on the poster but NOT in a disciple's MMT pathway, nothing was deleted and nothing moved. That rung is simply marked `trackable = false`: it belongs to the vision, but it is not ticked off one-by-one for each person.

### The two kinds of rung

- **Trackable rungs** are discrete and completable -- they have a moment of "done." *Finish BTLI 1. Take the gifts assessment. Attend Glorious Hope.* These appear on the disciple's checklist in MMT and count toward the progress bar.
- **Reference rungs** (`trackable = false`) are formational, continuous, or aspirational -- they have no finish line. *Basic prayer disciplines. Monthly discipler 1-on-1. Sharing your testimony. The fruit of the Spirit.* These live on the poster as the fuller picture of what we are becoming, but they are never reduced to a checkbox.

This is a Luke 6:40 design decision, not a technical accident. The goal is to become like Christ, not to finish a list. Putting "prayer disciplines" on a checklist with a circle to tick would teach a lie -- that prayer is a box you complete and move past. The checklist moves a person forward; the poster reminds everyone what the whole thing is *for*.

### How a leader "checks on" reference rungs

Not with a checkbox -- on purpose. Reference rungs are the shepherd's **conversation agenda**, not the app's scoreboard. When an LCL sits with a disciple, the poster is what they talk *around*: "You have finished BTLI -- how is your prayer life actually going? Are you still meeting your discipler monthly? Tell me about the last time you shared your testimony." None of that earns a green tick, because none of it has a finish line. **The app tracks what is completable; the leader shepherds what is not.** That division is the whole philosophy: the platform teaches the journey, the person walks it, and the LCL watches the parts a machine should not grade.

### What happens when the Pastor adds a rung

In the poster's EDIT mode, a newly added rung defaults to **`trackable = true`**. So unless you say otherwise, a new rung appears on every relevant disciple's MMT checklist AND enlarges their denominator. Two consequences to hold:

1. **Adding a trackable rung shifts live progress bars.** Add one trackable rung to Level 1 and every L1 disciple who was at `7 / 8` reads `7 / 9` on the next reload. Nothing broke -- the goalposts moved because you moved them. Do it deliberately, not mid-orientation.
2. **If the thing you are adding has no finish line, untick "Trackable" when you add it.** It then joins the poster as a reference rung, exactly like prayer disciplines and coaching, and never touches anyone's checklist.

The rule of thumb:

> **Completable?** Leave it trackable -- it becomes a step.
> **Lifelong or formational?** Untick trackable -- it becomes part of the vision.

### Editing rungs is safe -- history is never harmed

`pathway_progress` has no foreign key to `pathway_rungs` (the catalog is interpretation; the ledger is history). So toggling a rung's `trackable` flag, renaming it, or tombstoning it (`published = false`) never touches anyone's completions. Prefer **tombstone over delete** while the pipeline is still being validated -- a tombstoned rung is invisible, costs nothing in the denominator, preserves every completion, and comes back with one click. Moving a rung between *levels* is the one exception: that is a data migration (catalog and ledger must move together), never a poster edit -- ask for a proven migration.

*(Denominator note: MMT and MLT count only `published AND trackable AND not giving` rungs. The giving rungs are excluded per the giving doctrine -- see `GIVING_JOURNEY.md` and Invariant #334. The reference-poster rungs seeded from the static wall chart are `trackable = false` by design, which is why the progress bar divides by the small real number, not by every published rung.)*
